The portfolio problem

Every holding runs on its own owner.

You bought companies, not operating systems. Each one runs a different way, on a different stack, held together by a founder who is the only person who understands it. The value you underwrote is trapped in people who can walk, and you cannot see any of it until the monthly package lands.

That is concentration risk on every hold, and it is a discount at every exit. A buyer pays less for a company that depends on one person, and you feel it across the whole portfolio at once.
