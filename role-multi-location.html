The Problem

Inconsistency is the cost of scale.

The problem with multi-location operations isn't scale - it's inconsistency. Each site develops its own workflows, its own way of tracking jobs, its own crew habits. The data from five locations looks like data from five different companies. You can't benchmark it. You can't compare it. You find out a location is underperforming at month-end, not when it's happening.

TMI standardizes operations across every site. Same systems, same data capture protocols, same reporting format - so your numbers are comparable and your performance gaps are visible before they compound. One dashboard shows every location's revenue, utilization, job margins, and crew performance in real time.

You stop managing by exception when you finally hear about a problem. You start managing by signal - seeing what's actually happening across all sites, right now.

How It Works

Standardize. Compare. Improve.

01

Standardize across every site

The same operational systems deploy at every location. Dispatch, job tracking, field data capture, and invoicing run on the same platform everywhere - so data from site three looks exactly like data from site one, and you can actually compare them.

02

Live cross-location visibility

Revenue, utilization, job completion rate, and margins are visible across all branches in a single dashboard - updated in real time, not pulled from weekly reports. When one location's numbers change, you see it immediately.

03

Benchmark and close gaps

Sites are ranked against each other on the metrics that matter. When one location's crew utilization drops or its invoice cycle lengthens, the system flags it. You see the pattern and the gap - not just that something went wrong.
