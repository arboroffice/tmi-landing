The Operating System

The TMI OS.

Not a dashboard. Not a workflow tool. A multi-agent operating system that runs your field operation the way a full back-office team would — except it doesn't sleep, doesn't forget, and doesn't need to be managed.

45 Agents Custom Built Per Operation

One agent is a tool. Forty-five is an operation.

Most AI products are single-agent: one model, one task, one interface. You prompt it, it responds. That works for drafting emails. It doesn't work for running a field operation with 12 crews, 80 jobs in the queue, and a compliance audit next week.

The TMI OS is a swarm. Specialized agents running in parallel, handing off to each other, and escalating only when decisions exceed their authority. Dispatch passes to job costing. Job costing flags the project manager. The project manager is you — only when something actually needs you.

Every TMI build is a custom configuration of the OS. We identify which agents your operation needs, integrate them with your existing tools, and install the stack. It runs. You run your business.

The Agent Stack

What the OS runs.

Field Operations

Dispatch Agent

Assigns jobs to crews based on location, skillset, equipment, and availability. Communicates assignments via SMS. Handles reschedules and escalates conflicts.

Field Operations

Job Status Agent

Tracks crew check-ins, monitors job progress, and sends customer updates automatically. Flags delays and missed check-ins to the project manager.

Field Operations

Work Order Agent

Generates, routes, and closes work orders. Captures field notes, photos, and signatures. Triggers invoice creation on close.

Finance

Job Costing Agent

Tracks labor, materials, and equipment costs against budget in real time. Flags margin risk before the job closes. Generates post-job profitability reports.

Finance

Invoice Capture Agent

Monitors job close events and triggers invoice creation. Pulls job data, applies pricing rules, and routes for approval. Follows up on unpaid invoices.

Finance

Revenue Leakage Agent

Audits completed jobs against billed amounts. Identifies work performed but not captured. Surfaces unbilled items for review before they're lost.

Crew & HR

Time Tracking Agent

Manages crew clock-in/out via GPS and SMS. Cross-references location with job assignments. Flags discrepancies and generates payroll-ready timesheets.

Crew & HR

Compliance Agent

Monitors certifications, licenses, and training expiration dates across your entire workforce. Triggers renewals 30, 14, and 7 days before lapse.

Crew & HR

Safety Agent

Runs pre-job safety briefings via SMS. Captures incident reports. Monitors PPE compliance through connected camera systems. Maintains an audit-ready safety log.

Customer

Lead Follow-Up Agent

Runs automated multi-touch follow-up sequences over email and SMS. Qualifies inbound leads, books appointments, and hands off to the dispatch queue.

Customer

Inbound Call Agent

Handles first-call response, captures job details, and routes to dispatch. Works after hours. Never puts a customer on hold.

Customer

Estimation Agent

Generates preliminary estimates based on job type, historical data, and material costs. Drafts proposals for review. Speeds the quote-to-job conversion.
