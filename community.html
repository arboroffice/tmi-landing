Founders of the Future is TMI's network of owners and operators rebuilding their companies on systems and intelligence. The people who decided their business would run on more than memory and hustle, and who share what actually works with each other.

What this is

A room of operators building intelligent companies.

This is not a generic mastermind full of people selling courses to each other. It is a peer group of serious operators and owners who run real companies with crews, trucks, invoices, and payroll, and who are rebuilding those companies to run on systems and intelligence instead of the owner's head.

Everyone here has the same problem in a different shape. The business grew faster than the systems that were supposed to hold it together. The work of the community is figuring out, together, what the fixed version looks like, and then doing it.

Serious operators, building the modern version of their companies, in the same room.

What you get

What being in the room actually gives you.

The value is not a login and a course library. It is access to the letters, the gatherings, and the people who have already built what you are trying to build.

The letters

Founders of the Future letters

The Founders of the Future letters break down one operational problem at a time. What is quietly costing the business, why it happens, and what the system-built version looks like. Written for owners and field managers, not for tech people.

Together

Events and gatherings

Events where operators get in the same room, walk through what they built, and show the numbers behind it. You see the actual systems other companies are running, not a polished stage version of them.

Peers

A peer group who have done it

A group of owners and operators who have already rebuilt parts of their companies. When you hit a wall, someone in the room has hit the same wall and can tell you what they did and what it cost.

Playbooks

Cross-company intelligence and playbooks

What one company learns about job costing, dispatch, or accountability becomes something the whole group can use. The playbooks that work move between companies instead of dying inside one.

Who it is for

Founders and owner-operators serious about building.

This is for people who run a real company and have decided to build the modern version of it. Not people looking for another app, and not people looking for a networking group. People who want their business to run on systems that outlast any single employee, including themselves.
