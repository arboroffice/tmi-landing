The Problem

Where safety training companies lose the repeat business they already earned

Safety training is one of the most defensible recurring revenue businesses in the field services world. The frustrating part is how much of that renewal revenue walks out the door because no one has a system to catch it before it does.

01

Certifications That Expire Without Follow-Up

You trained 400 workers for a large construction company last year. Their certifications expire in 12 months. If no one sends a reminder at 90, 60, and 30 days, they'll book the next provider that calls first. You trained them but someone else gets the renewal.

02

Instructor Scheduling Complexity

Your instructors are certified for specific courses. A request comes in for 40 workers at a remote site. Matching the right instructor to the right course at the right location on the right date runs through multiple conversations, a spreadsheet, and two rounds of phone calls. For a business this predictable, that's too slow.

03

Compliance Records Scattered Across Systems

A client calls because OSHA is auditing their site and they need training records for 60 workers. Your records are in a filing cabinet, three email attachments, and a spreadsheet that someone updated last March. Producing that documentation takes a full day and still looks disorganized.
