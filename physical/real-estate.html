The Problem

Real estate operations run on spreadsheets and phone calls

The asset class is sophisticated. The operations behind it usually aren't. Maintenance, leasing, vendor management, and portfolio reporting all run on manual processes that don't scale.

01

Maintenance is reactive, not managed

Tenant requests come in through texts, calls, and emails. Work orders get created manually, vendors are called one by one, and nobody has visibility into status until a tenant calls again to complain.

02

Portfolio visibility doesn't exist

Occupancy, NOI, capital expenditure, and vendor spend across 20 properties lives in 20 different spreadsheets. Getting a real picture of portfolio performance takes days, not minutes.

03

Leasing and turnover is manual chaos

Move-outs, unit inspection, make-ready coordination, and lease-up all handled through phone calls and texted photos. Every turnover takes longer and costs more than it should.
