The Problem

The operational gap in mining

Remote sites, heavy equipment, rotating shifts, and strict compliance requirements create pressure that spreadsheets and disconnected software can't handle under production pressure.

01

Equipment failures at sites where downtime is catastrophic

When a haul truck or drilling rig goes down at a remote site, the cost isn't just the repair - it's production stoppage, crew idle time, and the logistics of getting parts and technicians in. Without predictive visibility, failures are always surprises.

02

Safety and compliance that can't fall through the cracks

MSHA requirements, environmental permits, incident reporting, and certification tracking - managed manually across shifts and sites with real legal and operational exposure when something isn't filed on time or filed wrong.

03

Shift coordination without a reliable system underneath it

Rotating crews, contractor headcount, equipment assignments, and production targets all coordinated by supervisors who are already managing a hundred things at once - on whiteboards, radios, and phone calls that don't create records.
