The Problem

3PLs are being measured against tech-forward competitors with real-time visibility and automated reporting.

The 3PLs that lose clients aren't doing worse work. They're providing less visibility and slower reporting than competitors who built better infrastructure.

01

Client visibility requires manual reporting

Your clients want to know their inventory levels, inbound shipments, and order status right now - not in the report you send on Fridays. Manual reporting creates a visibility gap that clients increasingly won't tolerate.

02

Billing reconciliation is complex and time-consuming

3PL billing involves storage fees, pick-and-pack charges, inbound receiving, and accessorials - each calculated differently per client contract. Manual invoicing takes hours and creates disputes.

03

Inventory accuracy requires constant physical verification

Cycle count discrepancies, receiving errors, and shipment exceptions create inventory inaccuracies that erode client trust. Manual reconciliation doesn't scale.
