The Problem

Coiled tubing revenue lives in the details - depth logs, fluid usage, and equipment time that manual processes consistently miss.

The operations that lose margin aren't running worse jobs. They're not capturing everything the job produced, and billing after the fact misses what happened.

01

Depth and run data requires manual transcription

Field logs are written by hand, carried back to the office, and entered into a system by someone who wasn't there. Transcription errors, missing entries, and delayed processing all reduce billing accuracy.

02

Tubing fatigue tracking is inconsistent

Tubing life depends on footage run, bend cycles, and pressure history. Most companies track this with spreadsheets that don't update automatically. Tubing failures are expensive and usually preventable.

03

Job billing requires data reconciliation from multiple sources

Fluid tickets, equipment time, depth logs, and standby reports all live separately. Assembling a complete invoice requires someone to pull from every source and calculate manually.
