The Problem

Locksmith companies lose emergency calls on response time and commercial contracts on follow-through.

When someone is locked out, the first tech to confirm arrival wins the job. And when you land a property management contract, the paperwork and billing that comes with it can kill the profitability if it's handled manually.

01

Emergency calls going to voicemail

Residential emergencies happen at midnight and on weekends. If your call handling depends on someone being awake and near the phone, you're losing the jobs with the highest margins to whoever answers first.

02

Commercial billing that never gets done cleanly

Property manager calls for a lockout, your tech goes, does the job, and the invoice lives in someone's head until end of month. Then you try to reconstruct 40 jobs from memory and texts.

03

No record of what was installed where

Rekeyed a building three years ago. New property manager calls and asks what keyway you used. You have no idea because it was written on a work order that's gone. Now you're starting over on a job you already did.
