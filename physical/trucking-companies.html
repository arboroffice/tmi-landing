The Problem

Trucking operations break down in the gap between the cab and the office.

The fleets that lose margin aren't driving badly. They're losing it to preventable maintenance, missed invoicing windows, and dispatch decisions made without real data.

01

HOS compliance is managed by memory and hope

Your drivers know their limits but the tracking system and the dispatch decisions don't always sync. A driver who gets an unexpected long haul when they're close to their limit creates an hours-of-service violation that's expensive and preventable.

02

Preventable breakdowns are eating maintenance budget

When trucks go down unexpectedly, the cost isn't just the repair - it's the missed load, the repositioning cost, and the customer call you don't want to make. Most fleets are still scheduling maintenance by mileage intervals instead of condition data.

03

Billing happens days after delivery

The load delivers Friday. The invoice goes out Tuesday after someone gets around to it. You're floating days of receivables on every load. In a margin-compressed business, that cash timing gap compounds fast.
