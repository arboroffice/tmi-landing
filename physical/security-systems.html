The Problem

Security installers lose recurring revenue when monitoring handoffs fall through the cracks.

System installed, customer happy, monitoring account never activated. Nobody caught it. You installed a system that's running dark and the monthly revenue never started. That's the most expensive kind of lost sale - you already did the work.

01

Monitoring account activation gaps after install

Technician installs the system, paperwork goes to the monitoring department, and somewhere in the handoff the account doesn't get activated. The customer assumes it's working. You don't know it isn't.

02

Permits that hold up installations in commercial jobs

Commercial security installations require permits in most jurisdictions. When the permit status isn't tracked, jobs get scheduled before the permit is approved, the tech shows up, and the work can't start. Labor wasted, customer frustrated.

03

Service call records that don't connect to equipment history

Panel trips, sensor failures, camera offline - service calls happen. When the tech dispatched to fix a recurring problem has no history of the previous three calls on that system, they start from scratch every time.
