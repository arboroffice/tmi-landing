The Problem

Three operational failures that cost fuel distributors contracts

Fuel and lubricant customers are sticky - until you let them down once. The operational failures that lose accounts are rarely about price. They're about reliability, documentation, and billing accuracy. All three are fixable with the right system.

01

Stockouts from Reactive Scheduling

Your delivery routes run on a fixed calendar. A customer's usage spikes because they're running two shifts for a big job. Their tank hits empty on a Wednesday morning. Emergency delivery dispatched at cost. Customer calls it "your second miss this year" and starts pricing competitors.

02

Environmental Compliance Gaps

Fuel distribution carries serious regulatory requirements - spill documentation, manifest records, driver certifications, equipment inspections. When an agency audits and your records are incomplete, the fines are immediate and the contract exposure is real.

03

Billing Disputes on Delivery Volume

A customer disputes a delivery ticket. They say their tank didn't receive the full load. Your driver has a paper manifest. Their gauge shows something different. Without delivery documentation that's precise and timestamped, the dispute goes wherever the louder party pushes it.
