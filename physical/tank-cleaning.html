The Problem

Tank cleaning is high-risk work run on clipboards and memory.

Confined space entry, hazardous residue, and rotating crew certifications create a compliance burden most operations handle reactively. One missed document costs more than a missed job.

01

Certification expiration surprises

Crew certifications for confined space, H2S, and respirator fit-test expire at different times. Without a tracking system, you find out on site - not before the job starts.

02

Permit documentation is a paper chase

Hot work permits, confined space entry logs, and pre-job safety checklists exist in manila folders across multiple trucks. An audit or incident investigation becomes a days-long search.

03

Job cost bleeds on rerun work

Tank jobs that go over scope because of unexpected residue or equipment issues rarely get properly recaptured. The extra hours and materials disappear into margin without a system to flag and charge them.

What TMI Builds

Six systems. One operating layer.

Built for tank cleaning operations where compliance, crew safety, and job profitability have to happen at the same time.

01

Certification Tracker

Every crew member's certifications logged with expiration alerts. No one goes on a confined space job unless their credentials are current - the system tells you before dispatch.

02

Digital Permit Management

Confined space entry permits, hot work authorization, and pre-job hazard assessments completed and stored digitally. Every job has a complete compliance record from start to close.

03

Job Scope Change Capture

When the job scope changes in the field, the crew logs it instantly. Extra residue, added cleaning passes, and equipment swap-outs get captured at the source and flow directly into the invoice.

04

Equipment Readiness Log

Vacuum trucks, pressure washers, and PPE tracked by last service date and current job assignment. Equipment that isn't ready doesn't get dispatched - the system flags it first.

05

Client Job History

Full history of every tank cleaned for each client - previous residue types, scope notes, crew assigned, and completion sign-offs. Quoting the next job starts with actual data from the last one.

06

Margin by Job Report

Labor hours, disposal costs, and equipment time rolled up per job automatically. See which client types and job categories are actually profitable - not just busy.
