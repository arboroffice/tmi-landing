The Problem

Janitorial companies lose contracts they should keep because they can't prove consistent service.

The client says the bathrooms weren't cleaned last Tuesday. Your supervisor says they were. No one has a time-stamped photo or a completion log. The client finds someone else at renewal.

01

No proof of work when clients complain

When a client calls to say a restroom wasn't cleaned or trash wasn't emptied, you have no documentation to check. The dispute becomes your word against theirs, and the client usually wins that argument.

02

Crew no-shows that nobody catches until the client calls

Crew member calls out sick, the coverage doesn't happen, and you find out at 9am when the building manager calls. The building wasn't cleaned. You're apologizing instead of managing.

03

Supply costs that eat margin on flat-rate contracts

You priced the contract based on estimated supply usage. Actual usage is 30% higher and nobody tracked it. You're cleaning at a loss and not finding out until month-end when the numbers don't add up.

What TMI Builds

Six systems. One operating layer.

Built for commercial janitorial companies managing 5 to 200 accounts. Connects crew operations, quality documentation, supplies, and client reporting in one place.

01

Crew Check-In and Completion Logging

Crew checks in at site arrival and checks out at completion. Timestamps and location attached to each visit automatically. When a client questions service, you pull the record in 30 seconds.

02

Photo-Based Quality Inspection

Supervisors or crew log photos of completed areas by zone. Inspection checklists completed per site per visit. Client can see service history on demand without calling your office.

03

Absence Coverage and Crew Alerts

When a crew member calls out, the system identifies the gap and alerts the supervisor immediately with replacement options. Sites don't get skipped because the information didn't reach the right person in time.

04

Supply Tracking by Account

Supplies issued tracked per building. Actual usage compared to contracted estimate monthly. Overages flagged before they become a conversation about repricing the account.

05

Client Portal and Reporting

Each client gets a portal showing their service history, inspection scores, and issue reports. Reduces inbound complaint calls and gives renewal conversations a foundation of documented performance.

06

Contract Renewal and Pricing Alerts

Contract end dates and CPI adjustment windows tracked automatically. Renewal conversations triggered 90 days out. Actual cost data pulled to support repricing conversations with real numbers.
