The Problem

Facilities managers spend most of their time on coordination that systems should handle automatically.

The facilities teams that handle more square footage per manager are the ones with automation running the routine coordination.

01

Multi-site vendor coordination is a constant communication burden

Coordinating HVAC service, janitorial, landscaping, and security vendors across multiple sites involves phone calls, emails, and follow-up that takes real time.

02

Compliance requirements have deadlines that get missed

Fire suppression tests, elevator inspections, HVAC certifications, and ADA compliance reviews all have deadlines. Tracking them manually means some get missed.

03

Equipment issues are reported after failure, not before

By the time a tenant reports a problem, the equipment has already failed. Monitoring and maintenance scheduling should surface issues before the failure.
