The Problem

The money leaves before you know it's gone.

Construction margins get planned in the estimate and eaten on the job. Not by one big thing - by dozens of small ones. Change orders that never got signed. Hours that were never logged. Subs that ran over and nobody caught it until it was too late to adjust.

01

You find out you're over budget at closeout

Budget vs. actual is a spreadsheet nobody updates until the job is done. By then the decisions that would have fixed it - scope, crew, subs - are already made and you're eating the loss. Cost overruns on construction jobs don't happen at the end. They happen in week three, when nobody is looking at the numbers.

02

Change orders become verbal agreements become disputes

The GC says they told the sub about the scope change. The sub says they never got a signed CO. The work is done and the money is gone. That conversation happens on every job because there is no system that catches scope changes in the moment and turns them into signed, logged change orders before the work starts.

03

One missing lien waiver holds up payment on a finished job

The project is done. The invoice is submitted. The owner holds payment because a lien waiver is missing from a sub who finished six weeks ago. Lien waivers, certified payroll, OSHA logs, and insurance certs tracked in filing cabinets and email chains create that situation on every project.
