The Problem

Managing 20 communities on spreadsheets and group texts is not a business. It is a liability.

01

Work Orders Fall Through Cracks

A resident submits a leak repair. It hits your email, gets forwarded, then nothing. Three weeks later they call the board. By then it is a bigger repair and an angrier board member.

02

Vendor Management Is a Phone Tag Loop

You have five vendors for landscaping alone across different properties. Getting quotes, approving work, and confirming completion happens through texts nobody can audit later.

03

Board Reporting Eats Your Friday Afternoons

Compiling what happened, what was spent, and what is pending for each community requires digging through inboxes and spreadsheets. It takes hours every month that should be billable time.

01
