The Problem

The business runs on recurring revenue that isn't recurring enough.

A gym or studio lives on member LTV. But most operations have no early warning system for churn, no consistent system for converting trials, and a front desk spending hours on calls that should never happen. Growth stalls because the leaks aren't visible until the damage is done.

01

Churn is discovered too late to do anything about it

A member who stops showing up in week two is going to cancel before week eight. The attendance data is there. Nobody is looking at it as a churn signal. The cancel request arrives and it's already too late for a re-engagement conversation.

02

Trial-to-paid conversion is inconsistent

A 2-week trial with no structured follow-up converts at 20 to 30%. The same trial with a consistent touchpoint sequence converts at 50 to 65%. Most gyms have no sequence. They have a staff member who's supposed to follow up when they remember to.

03

The front desk is managing calls that shouldn't exist

Schedule questions. Class cancellations. Membership holds. These calls eat hours every day. They're not complex. They're just not automated. Every hour the front desk spends on routine calls is an hour they're not converting leads or retaining members.
