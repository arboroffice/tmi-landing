The Problem

The operational gap in agriculture

Seasonal windows, dispersed equipment, labor complexity, and compliance requirements create pressure points that generic software was never designed to handle.

01

Equipment downtime during critical windows

A combine that goes down during harvest isn't just a repair — it's potentially tens of thousands in missed yield. Without visibility into equipment health, failures surprise you at the worst time.

02

Seasonal labor that's hard to coordinate

Managing crews across fields, shifts, certifications, and payroll during peak season is a scheduling and compliance challenge that spreadsheets and phone calls can't keep up with.

03

Compliance spread across too many systems

Food safety, environmental, and USDA requirements tracked manually — in email threads, binders, and disconnected apps — with real legal and financial exposure when something slips.

What We Build

AI systems built for agricultural operations

Equipment Health Monitoring

Real-time status tracking across tractors, combines, irrigation systems, and field equipment — with predictive maintenance alerts before failure windows open.

Seasonal Labor Coordination

Crew scheduling, certification tracking, and shift coordination automated across planting, growing, and harvest cycles — with payroll data captured in the field.

Compliance Automation

Food safety, environmental, and regulatory documentation generated automatically — FSMA, GAP, EPA chemical records — so audits don't require a week of prep.

Operational Intelligence

Yield data, cost-per-acre, input costs, and crop progress in one dashboard — accessible from the office or the cab. Real numbers while the season is still running.

Supply Chain & Inventory

Inputs, chemicals, seed, and parts tracked across locations — with reorder triggers, vendor coordination, and cost reconciliation handled automatically.

Field & Harvest Reporting

Field activity logs, spray records, yield reports, and incident documentation captured in the field and filed automatically — never manually re-entered.
