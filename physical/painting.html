The Problem

You're eating the difference between estimate and reality.

Most painting shops live on feel. Estimators who guess well. Crews who know the sequence. Owners who hold it all in their head. That works until it doesn't -- and when it breaks, it costs you the job margin.

01

Estimating surface area is part art, part science -- and most painters get it wrong

Irregular surfaces, multiple coats, prep time that runs long -- the gap between what was estimated and what was actually needed comes out of your margin. Most shops don't track estimate accuracy per job, so the same mistakes repeat.

02

Multi-crew, multi-job sequencing breaks without a coordination layer

Prep crew finishes ahead of schedule. Prime crew isn't available yet. Final coat crew shows up to a job that isn't ready. Running five crews across eight simultaneous jobs on a whiteboard means someone is always waiting -- on your dime.

03

Commercial accounts walk when nobody manages the relationship systematically

Property managers, HOAs, and facility directors give repeat business to whoever follows up. Most painting contractors win the first contract and lose the renewal because they're too busy to manage the relationship. That's a systems gap, not a sales problem.
