The Problem

Last-mile margins are thin and every inefficiency shows up directly on the P&L.

Failed deliveries, unoptimized routes, and manual dispatch decisions are the difference between profitable and losing money at scale.

01

Route planning is under-optimized and time-consuming

Manual routing takes time every morning and still leaves density gaps. A dispatcher planning 40 routes can't factor real-time traffic, time window constraints, and driver familiarity simultaneously. The result is wasted miles and overtime.

02

Failed deliveries create re-delivery costs and customer friction

A failed delivery attempt costs $15-25 in re-delivery expense and creates a customer service issue. Most operations don't have a system that proactively reduces failure rates through customer communication before the driver arrives.

03

Driver productivity is tracked after the fact, not in real time

You find out a driver had a bad day when you review the data tonight. By then the route is done, the overtime is incurred, and the complaints are in. Real-time visibility changes when you can intervene.
