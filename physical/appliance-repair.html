The Problem

Appliance repair companies bleed margin on every delayed part and missed follow-up.

Most shops run on tribal knowledge and sticky notes. When a tech misses a callback or orders the wrong part, you eat the cost. The work was done - the money just didn't show up.

01

Parts ordered wrong or too late

Techs diagnose in the field but parts orders go through a chain of calls and texts. Wrong parts show up, right parts don't, and the customer waits three days for a 30-minute fix.

02

Callbacks go cold before anyone follows up

Customer calls in, gets told a tech will follow up, and nobody does. That job went to the next shop in the search results. No system tracked it. No one knew it was lost.

03

Technician schedules built on guesswork

Routing five techs across a metro area without real-time visibility means two guys crossing town and one sitting idle. Labor costs climb while capacity sits unused.
