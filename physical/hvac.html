The Problem

HVAC operations break at the exact moment demand peaks.

Summer AC calls and winter heat emergencies arrive in waves. The shops that lose jobs aren't slower -- they're running on manual systems that can't scale when the phone doesn't stop ringing.

01

Skill-sensitive dispatch is a daily guessing game

You can't send just anyone to a commercial chiller or a brand-specific warranty call. Knowing which tech has the right cert, the right training, and the nearest position -- that's a problem most dispatchers solve by memory. Memory breaks when it's 95 degrees and 40 calls are queued.

02

Maintenance agreements are recurring revenue you can't actually track

You've got 300 maintenance customers on a spreadsheet. Some are annual, some are semi-annual, some are overdue by four months. The revenue is there -- but collecting it requires someone manually cross-referencing dates and making calls. Most shops lose 20-30% of that contract revenue to administrative gaps.

03

Emergency calls blow up planned schedules with no recovery path

A midnight heat-out call in July isn't optional. But inserting it into a full schedule means three other customers get pushed, no one is notified, and the dispatcher spends an hour on the phone explaining delays. There's no system -- just a dispatcher making it work.
