The Problem

Courier operations run on speed and accuracy - both of which break down when dispatch is manual.

The fleets that lose accounts aren't slower drivers. They're losing on response time, tracking visibility, and billing accuracy.

01

On-demand dispatch is reactive, not intelligent

When a pickup comes in, the right driver isn't always obvious - it depends on location, load, and what else is queued. Manual dispatch gets it right most of the time. AI gets it right every time, instantly.

02

Customers can't see where their package is

If a customer has to call to find out where their courier is, you're behind. Real-time tracking isn't optional anymore - it's expected.

03

Billing is manual and prone to errors

Courier billing involves mileage, wait time, fuel surcharges, and special handling fees - all calculated manually per job. Errors erode margin and create disputes.
