The Problem

Fleet repair operators lose contracts to predictability - clients expect vehicles in service, not in the shop unexpectedly.

The shops that win fleet contracts are the ones that reduce unexpected downtime. That requires data-driven maintenance, not reactive repair.

01

Reactive maintenance is more expensive than planned maintenance

An emergency roadside repair costs 3-5x what the same repair would cost on a scheduled PM visit. Fleet clients calculate this and evaluate their shop partners on it.

02

Fleet billing requires work order reconciliation across vehicles

A fleet client with 40 vehicles needs an invoice that breaks down by vehicle, by job type, and by contract. Assembling this from individual work orders takes hours.

03

Vehicle history is scattered across paper records and memory

Knowing what was done on a specific vehicle last visit, what's coming due, and what recurring issues have appeared requires records that most shops can't produce instantly.
