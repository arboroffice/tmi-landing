How It Works

TMI's role in
your deal.

TMI isn't a lender. We're the operator-side partner that helps you identify the right structure, prepare correctly, and get to capital faster than you would alone.

Step 01

Capital Assessment

A structured assessment of what you're trying to fund, your current financials, and which capital structures actually fit your situation. Takes about an hour.

Step 02

Structure Selection

TMI recommends the right capital type and lender for your deal. We have relationships with SBA lenders, equipment finance companies, and revenue-based providers across the country.

Step 03

Application Support

TMI prepares your package — financial documentation, business narrative, technology deployment plan. The part most operators get wrong is the story. We get it right.

Step 04

Close & Deploy

Once funded, the capital goes to work. TMI stays involved to make sure the deployment — equipment, technology, hiring — happens on the timeline the lender approved.

For Technology Companies

SBIR grants:
the underused path.

Most field operations companies don't think of themselves as technology companies. But if you're deploying AI, robotics, or sensor systems for energy, infrastructure, safety, or defense applications — you may qualify for SBIR funding that most of your competitors have never heard of.

Phase I SBIR grants are typically $150K–$300K for feasibility research. Phase II can reach $2M for development. And unlike a loan, it's not debt — you don't pay it back, and you don't give up equity.

TMI has helped field operations companies identify applicable programs across DOE, DOD, DOT, and USDA. The application process is intensive, which is why most companies don't pursue it. TMI handles the application.

Questions

Common questions.

Do I need to be a TMI Network member?
No. TMI Funding is open to any field operations company, not just network members. That said, Operator-tier members get priority access to our lender relationships and faster processing. If you're not a member and the deal gets complex, we may recommend joining.
Does TMI charge for the capital assessment?
The initial capital assessment is free. If TMI helps structure and prepare a full application, we charge a preparation fee that varies by deal size and complexity. We disclose the fee before any work begins.
How long does it take to get funded?
Equipment financing can close in 5–10 business days. Revenue-based capital in 1–3 weeks. SBA loans typically take 60–90 days from application to close. SBIR applications are a longer process — typically 6–12 months from submission to award.
What if I've been declined before?
Prior declines are common, especially for SBA applications where preparation quality matters. TMI reviews what went wrong and either structures the deal differently, targets a different capital type, or addresses the issues before reapplying. Most declines are fixable.
Do you take a commission or referral fee from lenders?
In some cases TMI receives a referral fee from lending partners — we disclose this when applicable. Our goal is to match you with the best lender for your deal, not the one that pays us the most. If there's a referral arrangement, you'll know before we make the introduction.
