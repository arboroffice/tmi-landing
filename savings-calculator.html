Where Waste Hides

The six places
field companies
lose money.

Most of the waste in a field operations company isn't in a single obvious place. It's distributed across six categories that compound each other.

18%
of field labor hours

Idle Time

Time between jobs, waiting on materials, re-work setup, and crew transportation gaps. Averaging 3–6 hours per person per week across most field operations.

12
average days billing delay

Billing Lag

Every day between job completion and invoice sent is a day you're financing completed work. On $3M revenue, 12 days of lag is ~$100K in float.

1 in 5
jobs with CO disputes

Change Orders

Without documentation, change orders are disputed. With documentation, they're paid. The gap is typically several thousand dollars per disputed job.

15 hrs
weekly office admin on manual tasks

Manual Admin

Scheduling, reporting, invoicing, and data entry that AI handles automatically. Usually the first thing to recover in a digital deployment.

8%
of materials cost

Materials Shrinkage

Missing materials, incorrect deliveries, and end-of-job inventory discrepancies. Adds up fast on large jobs without automated tracking.

$2,400
average unplanned repair cost

Reactive Maintenance

Equipment breakdowns during jobs cost more than the repair — they cost the delay, the crew idle time, and the customer relationship hit. Preventive tracking closes most of this.
