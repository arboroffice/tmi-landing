Questions

Before you start.

What is a digital office?

A digital office is a complete function of your company, like sales or dispatch, run by digital workers inside one system instead of assembled from separate tools. Instead of buying a chatbot, a scheduler, and a follow-up app, you install one sales office that answers, qualifies, books, follows up, and reports.

How is this different from buying software?

Software is parts you assemble and manage. An office is an outcome. TMI builds the whole function around your operation, your workflows, and your terminology, and every office shares one source of truth and one command center, so the company gets more intelligent each time you add one.

Can I start with one office?

Yes. Most owners start with the office that fixes the most expensive problem, usually sales, dispatch, or the back office, then stack the rest over time. The free audit tells you which office to install first.

Do I own what you build?

Yes. You own every office we build, the digital workers in it, the data they hold, and the decisions they make. There is no monthly software license and no lock-in.
