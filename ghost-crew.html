Ghost Crew

Deploy without
headcount.

On-demand robotic equipment and AI-staffed field capacity for field operations companies that need to scale a project without hiring a permanent crew.

Ghost Crew deploys when you need it, runs on the TMI OS, and disappears when the job is done. No full-time labor burden. No equipment debt.

What It Is

Scale without
the overhead.

Ghost Crew is a deployable operations unit — robotic equipment managed by AI agents, supervised by your team. Not a staffing solution. Not a rental company. An operational layer you turn on for a project and off when it's done.

Robotic Equipment

Autonomous and semi-autonomous equipment deployed on-site. Ground robots, aerial drones, inspection rigs, and sensor arrays from the TMI Hardware catalog. Configured for your job type before it arrives.

AI Staffing Layer

The agents that manage the equipment — dispatch, monitoring, documentation, exception flagging — run through the TMI OS. Your crew supervises. The AI handles the volume and the documentation.

Managed Deployment

A TMI deployment lead is on-site or on-call for the first 48 hours of every Ghost Crew deployment. You're not handed a manual. The system goes live with a person who can handle exceptions.

What Deploys

Equipment and
capabilities.

Ghost Crew configurations vary by job type. These are the capabilities that can be deployed — not all on every job, but available for the right applications.

Site Inspection & Monitoring

Aerial drones for site overviews, progress documentation, and safety compliance. Ground robots for confined space inspection and hazardous environment monitoring. All captures feed directly into the TMI OS documentation layer.

Survey & Mapping

LiDAR-equipped ground and aerial units for site surveys, as-built documentation, and topographic mapping. Data delivered in the format your team uses — not a new file format to decode.

Materials & Inventory

Automated inventory tracking, delivery verification, and materials documentation on large sites. Eliminates the missing materials problem and the end-of-day count discrepancies.

Safety Monitoring

Continuous PPE compliance monitoring, hazardous area detection, and incident documentation. Real-time alerts to site supervisors. Automatic incident reports for insurance and regulatory purposes.

Progress Documentation

Scheduled or continuous photo and video capture of site progress. Timestamped, GPS-tagged, and stored in the TMI Vault. Available for GC reporting, owner updates, and dispute resolution.

Environmental & Compliance

Air quality, soil condition, water monitoring for projects in regulated environments. Continuous compliance documentation that doesn't rely on someone remembering to take readings.

Use Cases

Where Ghost Crew
deploys.

Construction

Large Site Documentation

A GC needs daily progress photos across a 40-acre site for the owner and bank. Ghost Crew handles the entire documentation operation autonomously — timestamped, formatted, distributed daily.

Oil & Gas

Pipeline Inspection

Inspection robots run a pipeline corridor for anomaly detection and condition documentation. Data feeds into the operator's maintenance system automatically. No human entry into hazardous conditions.

Electrical

Transmission Tower Inspection

Aerial drones inspect tower structures and conductor runs at scale. Equipment that previously required a lineman at height is surveyed in hours with no exposure. Reports auto-generate for the utility.

Mining

Site Surveying & Mapping

LiDAR mapping of active mine sites for volume calculations, slope stability monitoring, and reclamation documentation. Delivered continuously instead of in quarterly survey bursts.

Industrial

Facility Inspection

Ground robots navigate industrial facilities for equipment condition inspection, safety compliance checks, and leak detection. Runs continuously or on schedule without pulling maintenance staff.

Trades

Project Closeout Documentation

Complete as-built documentation for project handoff — photos, measurements, and condition records captured in one automated sweep. Eliminates the punch-list photo problem.

How It Works

Four steps to
deployed.

01

Job Assessment

TMI reviews your project — scope, timeline, site conditions, and what you're trying to accomplish. Recommends the right Ghost Crew configuration for the job.

02

Configuration

Equipment is configured for your job type. Agents are loaded with your specific parameters — documentation formats, alert thresholds, reporting cadence. Tested off-site before deployment.

03

Deployment

Equipment arrives on-site. TMI deployment lead on-call for the first 48 hours. Your crew learns supervision protocols — typically a half-day session. System goes live.

04

Run & Remove

Ghost Crew runs for the duration of the project. Data flows into your systems throughout. When the project ends, equipment returns. No storage, no maintenance, no liability.
