What TMI Builds

Six systems. Built for the roofing business.

From storm response to supplement capture, every system is custom-built for how roofing operations actually run - not adapted from generic field service software.

Estimating

Satellite Instant Estimating

Pull roof measurements from aerial imagery, calculate square footage, pitch factors, and waste rates, and generate a line-item estimate in 90 seconds. No truck roll for initial measure. Feeds directly into proposal workflow.

Storm Response

Storm Lead Automation

When a hail or wind event hits your service area zip codes, the system generates a lead list from property records, triggers outbound contact sequences, and routes qualified leads to your sales team. Respond in hours, not days.

Insurance

Insurance Supplement Capture

Tracks every insurance job, compares approved scope against completed work, identifies supplement-eligible line items, and prepares documentation automatically. Stops the 25-40% of supplement revenue that typically goes unclaimed.

Scheduling

Multi-Crew Scheduling and Material Sequencing

Assigns jobs based on crew size, skill level, and geography. Ties material delivery schedules to install dates - so crews aren't waiting and material isn't sitting exposed. When jobs shift, delivery orders update automatically.

Documentation

Claims Documentation System

Captures photos, notes, measurements, and adjuster communications in structured format as the job progresses. Builds the claim file in real time - not after the fact. Supplement requests generate from the same data.

CRM

Customer and Referral CRM

After job completion, triggers review requests, referral incentive offers, and neighbor outreach in storm-affected areas. Referrals tracked through the pipeline so you see which jobs generate the most downstream revenue.
