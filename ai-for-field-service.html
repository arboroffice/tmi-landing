FAQ

Common questions about AI for field service.

The most impactful systems for field service are autonomous dispatch, same-day invoicing triggered at job close, and revenue leakage detection that catches unbilled parts and labor. Together these typically recover 5-15% of field revenue that currently goes uncollected.

Autonomous dispatch reads incoming job requests, matches them against technician skills, certifications, current location, and availability, then assigns and routes automatically. Techs receive job details on their phone. No dispatcher phone calls required for standard work orders. Emergency and priority jobs follow escalation rules you define.

Revenue leakage detection compares what was used on a job - parts, labor hours, travel time, consumables - against what was actually invoiced. It surfaces jobs where items were used but not billed, flags patterns by tech or job type, and triggers correction workflows. Most field service companies lose 5-15% of revenue this way.

When a tech marks a job complete in the field, the invoice generates automatically from the work order - parts, labor, and any add-ons - and sends to the customer with a payment link. The 9-day average invoice cycle compresses to same-day. Most companies collecting this way see faster payment and fewer disputes.
