What TMI Builds

Six systems. Built for how field service actually runs.

Not a generic platform you configure yourself. A complete operating infrastructure designed around your crew, your customers, and your service area.

Dispatch

Autonomous Dispatch

Reads incoming job requests, matches against technician skills, certifications, location, and availability, then assigns and routes automatically. No dispatcher phone calls for standard work orders. Emergency jobs escalate on rules you define.

Billing

Invoice and Payment Automation

When a tech marks a job complete, the invoice generates from the work order - parts, labor, and add-ons - and sends to the customer with a payment link. The 9-day average cycle compresses to same-day. Disputes drop because the record is complete.

Operations

Work Order Management

Work orders generate automatically from customer requests, route to the right tech, update as the job progresses, and close with all documentation attached. Nothing sits in a dispatcher's inbox or a tech's pocket waiting to be entered.

Revenue

Revenue Leakage Detection

Compares what was used on a job - parts, labor hours, travel, consumables - against what was actually invoiced. Surfaces every job where items were used but not billed. Flags patterns by tech and job type. Triggers correction workflows before the customer is out of reach.

CRM

Customer CRM and Follow-up

Tracks every customer, every job, and every service history in one place. Automated follow-up sequences after job completion. Review requests, maintenance reminders, and re-engagement for customers who haven't called in 12 months.

Compliance

Compliance and Crew Tracking

Monitors certifications, licenses, and insurance requirements for every technician. Expiring credentials trigger alerts. Dispatch routes only compliant techs to jobs requiring specific certifications. Liability exposure from lapsed credentials goes to zero.
