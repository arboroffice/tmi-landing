What every buyer asks

Six questions decide your multiple.

A buyer, a private equity firm, or the person you hand it to asks the same things. Every answer of no is a discount on the number they are willing to pay.

01

Does the business rely on the owner?

If the answer lives in your head, the value leaves when you do.

02

Are the systems documented?

A buyer pays for process they can read, not tribal knowledge they have to rediscover.

03

Can employees run it without you?

A team that already operates the company is the difference between an asset and a liability.

04

Is the data centralized?

If nobody can see the whole business in one place, due diligence turns into a fishing trip.

05

Can a new operator scale it?

Buyers pay for the next chapter, not just the last one. Scalable systems raise the ceiling.

06

Is there real operational visibility?

Predictability is what a buyer is buying. Chaos gets discounted, or passed on entirely.

The owner is usually the biggest risk in the business. We remove that risk.
