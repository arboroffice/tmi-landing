Questions

Things founders ask before they start.

How much of my time does this take?

Almost none beyond living your normal life. We film around your real days, so you are not adding hours of production. A few filming windows is usually all it takes, and we handle every other part: editing, planning, posting, and strategy.

Do I have to be good on camera?

No. The best founder content is real, not polished. Our creator knows how to capture you being yourself and how to cut it so it lands. You do not have to perform or memorize anything.

Who owns the content?

You do, completely. Every clip, every edit, every piece we create is yours to keep and use however you want, forever.

What platforms do you post on?

The ones that matter for your audience. Usually short-form video on Instagram, TikTok, YouTube, and LinkedIn. We build the calendar around where your buyers and talent actually spend time.

How fast will I see results?

Content starts going out within about two weeks. Attention builds over the first months as the brand finds its voice and the right clips hit. This is a compounding asset, not an ad you switch on and off.

Is there a long contract?

No long lock-in. We earn the next month by making the last one worth it. The work compounds, so most founders stay because the brand keeps getting stronger, not because a contract makes them.
