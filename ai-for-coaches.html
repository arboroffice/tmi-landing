The Problem

Your expertise scales. Your operations do not.

Coaching and consulting businesses hit the same wall. The founder generates leads, delivers results, and manages the business. When all three depend on the same person, growth means working more hours - not building something that compounds.

01

Leads that go cold while you are in sessions

Someone fills out your application or downloads your lead magnet at 2pm on a Tuesday. You see the notification at 7pm after your last call. By then they have already heard back from someone else, or the moment of enthusiasm has passed. The conversion that was available at 2pm is gone by 7pm.

02

Onboarding that depends on the founder being available

A client pays. Now someone needs to send the welcome email, share the intake form, send the scheduling link, grant community access, and follow up if anything is not completed. When all of that lives in the founder's head and calendar, every new client is a time event for the person who should be focused on delivery.

03

Launches that run on manual effort and adrenaline

Open cart for five days. Dozens of email sends, social updates, webinar reminders, deadline emails, and objection-handling sequences - all managed manually during the exact window when you are also doing live calls and handling inbound questions. The launch works when the founder is at full capacity. When they are not, revenue suffers.

04

Revenue that is not visible until the spreadsheet gets updated

How many active clients. What the monthly recurring revenue looks like. Which cohort has the highest renewal rate. Which lead source converts to paid at what rate. Most coaching businesses can answer none of these questions without opening three platforms and assembling the data themselves - which means decisions are made on gut feel, not information.

Lead response, onboarding, launch mechanics, and renewal systems built around how coaching and consulting businesses generate and retain revenue - not adapted from a B2B sales playbook.

Lead Response and Follow-up Automation

Contacts new leads within 60-90 seconds of submission via email or SMS, personalized to the source and offer. Follow-up sequences run on a defined schedule based on engagement. Leads never fall through a gap because someone was in a session.

04

Launch Sequence Automation

Email sequences, webinar reminders, cart open notifications, objection-handling messages, and deadline sequences run on your launch calendar automatically. The launch runs on the system during the exact window when you are also doing live events and sales calls.

05

Revenue and Pipeline Dashboard

Renewal and Retention Automation

Common Questions

What coaches and consultants ask before they start.

Lead response automation and client onboarding systems deliver the fastest and most measurable ROI. Research shows a 9x conversion difference between responding to a lead in under 5 minutes versus over 30 minutes. Most coaching businesses respond in hours, not minutes - because the founder is in sessions when the lead comes in. Fixing that gap alone changes close rates significantly before anything else in the funnel is touched.

TMI builds a lead response system that contacts new leads within 60-90 seconds of submission via email, SMS, or both - with a message that is personalized to the source they came from and the offer they responded to. If the lead does not respond to the initial contact, follow-up sequences continue on a defined schedule based on engagement signals. Leads never fall through a gap because the founder was in a session or traveling.

TMI builds onboarding workflows that trigger the moment a client completes payment. Welcome sequence, intake form delivery, session scheduling link, access credentials for your community or course platform, and first session preparation instructions all dispatch automatically in the right order without the founder or an assistant managing each step. A launch sequence is the series of emails, social prompts, webinar reminders, cart open notifications, objection-handling messages, and deadline sequences that drive enrollment in a cohort or program. TMI builds these as automated systems that run on your launch calendar without requiring you to manually send or schedule each piece during the days when you are also running live events, handling sales calls, and answering questions from prospects in real time.
