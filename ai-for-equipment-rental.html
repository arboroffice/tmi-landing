What TMI Builds

The entire equipment rental operation, on one system.

Not six point tools bolted together. One operating system that runs every lane, from the first reservation to collected cash, built for how a rental yard actually runs.

Front office

Availability & Utilization

Every asset shows live, on rent, available, in service, or sitting idle past its expected turn, by class and yard. When a machine idles longer than its class average, it surfaces instead of bleeding quietly.

Front office

Quotes & Reservations

Reservations check against real availability before they are promised, so you never double-book a unit. When demand outstrips available units, the system flags the reallocation before you turn business away.

Front office

Contracts & Rates

A confirmed reservation becomes a live contract with the right rate, term, and customer details carried through. No re-keying, and the floor and the office work off the same agreement.

Field

Delivery & Pickup

Open contracts, available units, and truck capacity sequence into full routes instead of one-off trips, leaving from the yard that cuts deadhead miles. Returns get scheduled for turnaround without a dispatcher chasing by phone.

Field

Maintenance & Inspection

Engine hours and fault codes schedule service in the gap between contracts, not on a customer site. Every unit due back gets inspected and serviced before it goes out, so maintenance happens on idle time.

Field

Parts Inventory

Parts and consumables track against the units that consume them, with reorder triggers set to vendor lead times. A machine due back does not sit waiting on a part that should have been on the shelf.

Money

Recurring Rental Billing

Rentals re-rate when they run past term, off-rent units close out, and fuel, damage, and meter overages get captured. Every gap between what was billed and what was used surfaces before the invoice goes out.

Money

Asset ROI Costing

Hours, rentals, repairs, and revenue post per unit against cost to own. You see which machines still earn and which bleed maintenance dollars past their useful life, so repair-or-retire is a data call.

Money

Collections & Deposits

Deposits, open invoices, and damage charges age in one view with the contract attached. The system flags what is past terms so receivables and recoverable charges get worked before they slip.

Compliance

Insurance & Condition Docs

Check-out and check-in condition is captured with photos and inspection data tied to the asset and contract, alongside customer insurance and certificate records. Damage gets matched to the customer with evidence.

People

Customer History

Every contract, payment, damage claim, and dispute follows the customer across rentals. Who pays late, who returns units rough, and who is worth the next reservation lives on the record, not in memory.

Command

One Dashboard

Utilization, contracts, the yard, and the shop pull into one daily picture. Ownership sees where the fleet stands and what is earning without walking the yard or pulling from three systems.

Questions about AI for equipment rental.

Utilization intelligence consistently delivers the highest ROI for rental companies. Every machine sitting in the yard instead of out on a contract is dead capital, and most fleets run far below the utilization they could. Surfacing the assets that are idle, the ones that are due back, and the demand they could be filling is the single biggest lever on rental margin. Predictive maintenance between rentals is the second highest return - a machine that fails on a customer site costs you the repair, the swap, and the relationship.

When a machine has been idle longer than its class average, it surfaces. When demand for a class outstrips available units, it flags the reallocation before you turn business away.
