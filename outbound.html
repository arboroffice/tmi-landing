Questions

Things people ask before they start.

Can we use multiple call services at the same time?

Yes, and most clients do. A roofing company might have us doing cold outreach to new homeowners, post-purchase follow-up after every completed job, and a reactivation campaign to past clients, all at once. Each call type has its own script and list. One caller team handles all of it.

Do I have to manage the callers?

Zero operational work on your end. We hire, train, manage, and replace callers. You get a weekly summary and a live dashboard. That is it. We handle everything that happens before a meeting lands on your calendar.

How is this different from an automated follow-up tool?

A human call and an automated email are not the same thing. Automated tools send messages. Our callers have conversations. Someone who gets a personal call after their job is done feels valued. Someone who gets an automated text feels processed. The conversion rates are not comparable.

How long until we see results?

Callers are dialing within 14 days of signing. Post-purchase and reactivation campaigns often show results in the first week because we are calling people who already know the business. Cold outreach typically produces first bookings in weeks two and three. A full month gives you a clear run rate.

Is cold calling legal?

Yes, when it is done correctly. We scrub every list against the Do Not Call registry before dialing. We call business numbers during business hours using real human callers, and in most cases we are calling businesses, not consumers, which carries different rules. We do not use AI auto-dialers, which is where companies get into serious legal trouble.

What if the results are not what we expected?

We have a 60-day guarantee. If we do not hit the minimum meeting or follow-up volume we committed to in your first 60 days, we keep working at no additional charge until we do. After that it is month to month. We do not lock you into a long contract because we expect the results to keep you.
