Inside the Network

What happens
inside.

The TMI Network is built around things that actually move operations forward — not content calendars and engagement metrics.

Weekly Member Calls

Every week, a live call with 10–30 operators. One featured company walks through what they're building, what broke, and what's working. Open Q&A. Recorded for members who can't attend live.

Peer Operator Directory

A searchable directory of verified operators — filterable by trade, company size, geography, and systems they're running. Real companies. No vendor profiles.

Resource Library

The Vault, fully open. Ops audit frameworks, industry build guides, monthly AI in field ops reports, RFP templates, and the TMI Build Library — documented deployments from real companies.

Build Series

Deep-dive case studies published monthly. A real company, their real problem, what they built, and the actual numbers. Long-form, editorial, no vendor advertising.

Industry Channels

Organized by trade — construction, HVAC, electrical, oil & gas, landscaping, and more. Ask questions, share problems, get answers from people doing the same work.

Events Access

Members get early access and discounted tickets to TMI Summit. Partners get included tickets. Operators get VIP access to all TMI events including closed roundtables.

Questions

Common questions.

Who gets in?
Free and Member tiers are open to any operator in a field-based business. Partner and Operator tiers require an application — we screen for operational intent, not company size. We turn down vendors, consultants, and people who aren't actually running field operations.
What's the revenue share in Operator tier?
Operator members pay $1,000/month plus a 3% revenue share on new business generated through TMI introductions, referrals, and marketplace activity. It's a co-growth structure — TMI only benefits when you do. The 3% applies to the first year of new revenue from TMI-sourced relationships.
Can I cancel anytime?
Monthly tiers cancel anytime. Annual tiers are non-refundable after 30 days. Operator tier has a 90-day minimum engagement — enough time to actually see whether the partnership is working.
Is the community active?
We cap community size to keep the signal-to-noise ratio high. When the Operator channel has 400 members having shallow conversations instead of 80 members having real ones, we've built something worthless. We grow slowly on purpose.
What's included in the annual price?
Member annual is $2,500 (saves about $1,060 versus monthly). Partner annual is $5,000 (saves about $1,000). Operator doesn't offer an annual rate — the engagement is dynamic and we price it accordingly.
