What the platform holds

Nine capabilities in one connected layer.

Each of these grows every time a company joins the ecosystem. Nothing is discarded at the end of a project. It is captured, structured, and made reusable for the next build.

01

Cross-company operational benchmarks

Real performance ranges pulled from how comparable companies actually run, so you know what good looks like before you spend a dollar building toward it.

02

AI models trained on real business operations

Models shaped by live operating data from real companies, not generic text scraped off the internet. They understand how work actually moves through a business.

03

Industry playbooks

The specific sequence of moves that worked inside an industry, written down and ready to reuse instead of rediscovered from scratch every time.

04

Executive decision support

Structured answers for the calls owners have to make, grounded in what happened when operators in the same position made the same choices before them.

05

Predictive analytics

Early signals on demand, cash, capacity, and churn, drawn from patterns that only show up once you can see across many operations at once.

06

Knowledge graphs

A connected map of how people, processes, customers, equipment, and systems relate inside a business, so context is never trapped in one person's head.

07

Automation templates

Prebuilt workflows for the repeatable work every operation runs, ready to drop into a new company instead of engineered again from zero.

08

Human performance insights

What actually moves focus, energy, and output in the people running and working in the business, measured across real teams rather than assumed.

09

Enterprise dashboards

One screen that shows the true state of the whole operation in real time, built on the same shared definitions used across every company in the network.
