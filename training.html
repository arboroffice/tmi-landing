Delivery Formats

Three ways
to train.

On-Site

TMI trainers come to your facility. Training happens on your equipment, with your systems, in your environment. Crew Foundation sessions and Leadership track can both be delivered on-site. Minimum 10 participants for on-site delivery.

Cohort Events

Join a scheduled cohort at a TMI facility or Summit event. Crew Foundation half-days run monthly. Leadership 2-day sessions run quarterly. Certification cohorts run twice a year. You get peer operators in the room, not just your own team.

Remote / Hybrid

Crew Foundation and Leadership tracks are available remote with live facilitators and breakout sessions. Not the same as watching a recording — live instruction with real Q&A. Certification is in-person only.

Questions

Common questions.

Do we need TMI software to do the training?
No. The Crew Foundation and Leadership tracks are platform-agnostic — they teach the principles and workflows, not a specific interface. If you're using a specific system, TMI can customize examples to match it. The certification is platform-agnostic by design.
How long does a crew foundation session take?
Half day — typically 9am to 1pm. Long enough to cover everything a field worker needs to know to function in a digital operation. Short enough that you're not pulling crews off the job for more than a morning.
Can we customize the content for our operation?
Yes, for on-site delivery. TMI does a pre-training session with your management team to understand your specific systems, workflows, and problem areas. The content is adapted before the crew session happens.
What's the difference between Leadership training and TMI Certified?
Leadership training is a two-day operational course — practical, focused on making better decisions with your current systems. TMI Certified is a five-day certification program that ends with an assessed credential. Certified covers more ground and produces a verifiable qualification. Leadership training is the right choice for companies that want training without the certification overhead.
