Why good tradespeople stay employees

The trade was never the hard part. The business around it is.

You can install the system, pour the pad, wire the panel, or run the crew better than the boss you work for. But going out on your own means answering the phone at dinner, writing quotes at midnight, chasing checks that are sixty days late, and figuring out where the next job comes from when the calendar goes quiet. That part has nothing to do with your skill, and it is the part that sends most people back to a paycheck. And if you do not have a trade yet, the door looks closed before you even start.

The best tradesperson on the truck does not lose to a better tradesperson. They lose to a business they never had time to build. And the person who could have been great never got shown the door in.

How the partnership is structured

We only win when the business wins.

This is not a franchise fee and it is not a monthly bill that shows up whether you work or not. We tie our pay to the company we help you build, so our incentive and yours point the same direction from day one. Grow the business and everyone earns more. That is the whole idea.

Revenue share

A share of the revenue the business generates while we run the back office and drive the work to you.

Profit share

A share of the profit above an agreed baseline, so we are paid on what actually reaches your pocket.

Partner equity

A real ownership split in the company we build together, structured so the operator holds the majority.

Milestone step ups

Terms that improve as you hit revenue and crew milestones, so both sides stay pointed at growth.

The exact structure depends on the trade, the market, and what you bring to the table. We lay it all out in plain language before anyone commits to anything.

Common questions

Before you apply.

What if I don't have a trade yet?

That is fine. You do not need to already be a tradesperson to apply. If you are willing to learn and put in the work, we help you pick the right trade for your market and get you trained, certified, and job ready before we build the business around you. What you bring is the drive to show up and do the work. We handle the path in.

Do I need money to start?

You need to be able to cover your own tools, your license, and your day to day while the first jobs come in. You do not need to fund a marketing budget, hire an office team, or buy software. We build and run that side as the partner, and our pay is tied to the business growing, not to an upfront check.

What trades do you work with?

Skilled field trades where a good operator can build a real book of work: HVAC, plumbing, electrical, roofing, concrete, painting, landscaping, restoration, and similar home and commercial service trades. If you are not sure which trade fits you, apply and ask. We will tell you straight.

Do I own the business?

Yes. This is a partnership, not a franchise. You are the operator and, in most structures, the majority owner of the company we build together. We run the back office and share in the upside we help create. The exact split is written out in plain terms before anyone signs.

What do I actually do day to day?

You do the trade. You show up to the jobs we book, do work you are proud of, manage the site and eventually your crew, and tell us your capacity. Everything else, the calls, the quotes, the invoices, the marketing, the numbers, runs on our side.

How do I get started?

Apply through the form. We will have a straight conversation about your trade, your market, and your goals, then lay out exactly how the partnership would work for you. No pressure and no cost to find out if it is a fit.
