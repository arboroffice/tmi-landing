Acquisition

Three ways
to deploy.

Not every company needs to own the equipment. TMI offers purchase, lease, and Ghost Crew deployment depending on your project structure and capital situation.

Purchase
Own the equipment outright. TMI configures, delivers, and provides initial training. Best for companies with continuous need and capital available. Equipment financing available through TMI Funding.
Lease
36–60 month leases with buyout option. Monthly payment instead of capital outlay. Includes maintenance and software updates. Best for companies building a robotics practice over time.
Ghost Crew
Rental by project through the Ghost Crew program. No capital commitment, no maintenance. TMI deploys, operates, and removes the equipment. Best for occasional or specialized deployments.
