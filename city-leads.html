What a City Lead does

You open the market. The system does the rest.

You are not the pitch, the demo, or the build. You find the owners in your city who are running on people catching everything by memory, help them see where their operation leaks time and money, and get them in the room. Our team runs the call and builds what they need.

01 / OPEN

Own your city

You are the face of TMI in your market. You know the owners, the industries, and the way business actually gets done where you live. You open doors nobody else can.

02 / CONNECT

Find the leak

You talk to owners the way you would if you owned the place. You help them feel where the operation is losing money, then get them booked onto a call with our team.

03 / HAND OFF

Let the work speak

Our founder and strategists run the call and map the operation live. TMI builds the intelligent backend and digital workforce. You brought them the door. We walk through it together.
