The trap

Great software, run by a company that still scales on headcount.

01
Growth means hiring, not leverage
Every new customer adds support, onboarding, and success load. The only lever you have is more people, so margins shrink exactly as you scale.
02
Agents bolted on, not built in
You shipped a chatbot or an automation, but it has no memory, no permissions, no monitoring. It is a feature, not a workforce, and it does not compound.
03
Knowledge trapped in people and tools
Customer context, product decisions, and hard-won lessons live in heads and scattered tools. When someone leaves, the company forgets.
