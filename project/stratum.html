The shift

The trades are about to be rebuilt -with the work, not on top of it.

For two decades, software treated the trades as an afterthought. Office tools forced into the field. Forms where there should have been intelligence. Dashboards nobody opens.

That ends now. AI is the first technology since electricity that meets the work where the work happens - on the rig, in the panel, under the truck, on the line. It can see what no team can see, decide in seconds what used to take days, and act on what it knows without waiting for a Monday meeting.

We are building the systems that turn that capability into results you can put on a page - fewer incidents, more uptime, faster jobs, real margin. Not another tool. The operating layer the next generation of operators will run on.

What changes
03

The advantages that mattered yesterday don't matter tomorrow.

AI is rewriting the rulebook of competitive advantage. Operational scale, large teams, and expensive marketing fade. A different set of moats is rising in their place.

01
The new moats

Trust, brand, IP, and direct customer relationships become the asset.

As AI-generated content floods every channel, trust is the only thing that scales. Patents, trademarks, copyrights, proprietary data, AI-fluent talent - these are the moats of the next decade. Operational scale alone is not.

02
Spend shifts from people to tech

Smaller, more highly-paid teams. Tech spend rises sharply.

Aggregate compensation drops, but per-employee cost rises because the people you keep are elite. Tech budgets climb. HR, marketing, and customer support spend falls as agentic systems perform those tasks. The savings get reinvested.

03
IT becomes the foundation, not the bottleneck

Business units own and deploy AI. IT keeps the runway clear.

IT maintains the data, tech, and cyber backbone. But the people closest to the work - operations, field, finance - own and ship the AI solutions. Autonomy moves down. Speed goes up.

04
Lean, elite teams

Hierarchies flatten. Specialists and agents do the work.

Back-office processes run on agentic systems with human oversight. Skepticism gives way to embrace once the speed becomes obvious. AI takes the toil out of work - high performers ship more and enjoy it more.

What we commit to
06

Traceability is the spine. Everything else hangs from it.

The trades run on a chain of evidence. Who did the work, when, on what asset, against which spec, with which materials, signed off by whom. When that chain is whole, everything else - safety, compliance, audit, customer trust - gets easier. When it's broken, nothing works.

Our first commitment

Every action, asset, signature, and decision traced and queryable in one place.

The core of what we build is a single ledger of the work. Field actions captured at the source. Documents and inspections tied to the asset, not the inbox. Agentic decisions logged with their reasoning. Conversations attached to the job they're about. Whether the question comes from a regulator, an insurer, a customer, or your own crew next Tuesday - the answer is one query away, with the chain intact.
