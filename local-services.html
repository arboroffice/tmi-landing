What's costing you

Three problems every local service business deals with.

01
Dispatch runs through one person
Every job assignment is a phone call. Scheduling lives on a whiteboard or in someone's head. When that person is out, everything slows down or stops.
02
Revenue leaks between the job and the invoice
Extra work gets done and never makes it to the bill. Change orders get verbal approval and never get written down. You complete $12k of work and invoice $9k.
03
Customer follow-up happens whenever someone remembers
Review requests, maintenance reminders, referral asks - all manual, all inconsistent, all dependent on someone having time. Most of them never happen at all.
