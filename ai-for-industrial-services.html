What TMI Builds

Six systems. Built for industrial service operations.

Custom infrastructure that connects work orders, dispatch, compliance, job costing, and field data into one operating layer your team does not have to chase across inboxes, spreadsheets, and radios.

Operations

Work Order Automation

Captures every work order from intake to invoice across all your plants, sites, and clients. Requests are logged in one place, routed to the right crew, tracked through completion, and flagged for billing automatically. When a job stalls or a callback gets missed, the system surfaces it before the revenue ages out instead of letting it disappear into someone's inbox.

Scheduling

Technician Dispatch and Scheduling

Sees every technician, crew, and open job across all your sites at once. Assignments account for location, certification, equipment, and current workload, so the right qualified crew gets routed to the right job with travel time factored in. When an emergency call or turnaround shifts priorities, the schedule reflows and affected crews are notified automatically.

Compliance

Compliance and Safety Document Tracking

Tracks technician certifications, safety training, equipment inspections, permits, and client-required documentation tied to each job and each site. Expiring credentials are flagged before they lapse, and a crew missing a required certification cannot be dispatched to a site by accident. When a client or regulator asks for records, you assemble them in minutes.

Costing

Job Costing and Margin

Captures labor hours, materials, equipment, and subcontractor costs against each job as the work happens, not weeks later from memory. You see real margin per job, per site, and per client while the job is still open. An underwater job surfaces before it finishes - not at month end when the loss is already locked in.

Field

Field Data Capture

Pulls job notes, photos, readings, signatures, and completion data straight from the field into the system as the crew works. No paper packets reconstructed at the end of the week, no detail lost between the site and the office. The information that drives billing, compliance, and the next visit is captured once, at the source, and stays attached to the job.

Knowledge

Operational Memory

Holds the site history, equipment specifics, client requirements, and crew knowledge that usually lives in one veteran technician's head. When that person is out or moves on, the next crew still knows the access procedure, the known issues, and what was done last time. The operation stops depending on who happens to remember.
