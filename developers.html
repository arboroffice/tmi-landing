Developer Platform

Build on TMI.

Submit tools to the TMI marketplace, earn revenue share from live field operations, and get access to the operational data no other platform gives you.

API Access Revenue Share

Field operations have a data problem. You can help fix it.

The trades have been underserved by software for thirty years. The tools that exist were built for offices, not for people running crews in the field. Most of them don't talk to each other. None of them were built around AI-first architecture.

TMI is building the infrastructure layer. If you build tools for field operations - or want to - this is where you get access. We open the stack to builders who want to make field operations work better, and we share the revenue when their tools land inside a live operation.

This isn't an app store with a 30% cut. It's a partnership. We send you real customers who already have the infrastructure to use what you build.

What's Available

What you build on.

Access

TMI API

Read and write access to operational data across connected TMI deployments - job logs, crew data, dispatch records, field sensor feeds. Scoped per client with proper authorization.

Distribution

Tool Marketplace

Submit a tool and we list it across the TMI operator network. If an operator adopts it as part of their build, it's integrated into their TMI OS and you earn revenue share for the duration of the engagement.

Opportunity

Bounty Board

TMI operators post specific problems they need solved. You propose a build. If it's accepted and deployed, you're paid on delivery plus ongoing share. No cold outreach, no enterprise sales cycles.

Data

Field Intelligence Feed

Anonymized, aggregated operational data from field deployments. Useful for training models, benchmarking tools, and understanding how real operations actually run - not how vendors say they run.

Support

TMI Certified Dev Program

Technical onboarding, API documentation, sandbox environments, and a direct line to the TMI engineering team. Certified developers get priority placement in the marketplace.

Partnership

Co-Build Program

TMI identifies the highest-priority gaps in our operator network and works with external developers to build them. Shared IP, shared revenue, and a guaranteed first customer.

Revenue Model

How you get paid.

Bounties

Fixed delivery payment

Operators post a problem and a budget. You build, we validate, they pay. Payments are released on successful deployment into a live operation.

Marketplace

20% revenue share

When a TMI operator adds your tool to their stack, you earn 20% of the tool's annual contract value for as long as it's active. No one-time fees - ongoing share.

Co-Build

Negotiated per engagement

Co-build deals are structured individually based on scope, IP arrangement, and expected deployment. Minimum 15% revenue share plus a guaranteed first operator.

"We don't want a marketplace. We want a partner network."

What we need from you is the tool that solves the problem. We handle the integration, the deployment, and the relationship.
