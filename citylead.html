What is a City Lead

The TMI franchise without the franchise

A City Lead is TMI's boots on the ground in a specific market. You're not a salesperson. You're the operator who knows the business owners in your city - across the trades, field service, healthcare, professional services, e-commerce, hospitality, whatever your market runs on - and you introduce them to infrastructure that actually works.

You handle the local relationships. TMI handles the implementation, the system build, and the ongoing support. You get paid every time a client you brought in stays on.

This is for people who understand how real businesses run, have genuine networks in their market, and want a recurring revenue model tied to something that actually solves problems.

Exclusive territory
One City Lead per metro. No internal competition, no stepping on each other's territory.
You get paid on every deal
A cut of every deal you bring in. Not a one-time referral fee, you get paid on the business you generate, for as long as it runs.
Full implementation support
You don't build anything. TMI's team does the system work. You own the relationship and collect the revenue.
TMI-trained
We train you on the system, the pitch, the objections, and the client profile so you walk in ready.
Direct access to TMI
City Leads get a direct channel to the team for deal support, pricing questions, and client escalations.
Scalable income
The more clients you bring in, the more you earn - without adding complexity to your own operation.
