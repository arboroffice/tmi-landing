## Shared Direction

Use case: ads-marketing
Asset type: square Facebook ad source photograph
Primary request: Create a premium documentary-style industrial operations photograph for TMI, an AI infrastructure company for field and industrial businesses.
Style/medium: photorealistic editorial photography, credible working environment, contemporary equipment, no staged stock-photo smiles
Composition/framing: 1:1 square, subject and operational detail concentrated on one side, generous calm negative space on the opposite side for a text panel, clear depth, no split-screen
Lighting/mood: bright overcast or soft daylight, clean and optimistic, high-key but realistic
Color palette: warm off-white, charcoal, steel gray, restrained industrial colors, one very subtle chartreuse safety detail where natural
Constraints: no text, no logos, no watermark, no readable brand names, no fake UI, no dramatic disaster, no neon, no dark cinematic grade
