What it means

Most companies run on the owner. Certified ones run on systems.

A certified company does not lose its knowledge when someone leaves. It does not stall when the founder takes two weeks off. That is what a buyer pays a bigger multiple for, and what makes the company easier to grow, pass down, or sell.
