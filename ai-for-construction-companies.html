What TMI Builds

Six systems. Built for how construction actually runs.

Not Procore out of the box. A custom-built operating infrastructure designed around your project types, your subcontractor relationships, and how your field teams actually work.

Finance

Real-Time Job Costing

Labor hours, material purchases, and equipment time captured as they happen - not at month end. See estimated vs. actual margin mid-project. Alerts fire when a phase goes over budget before it compounds.

Change Orders

Change Order Capture and Approval

Captures scope changes in the field, generates formatted change order requests with pricing, routes them for owner approval, and updates the project budget automatically when approved. Nothing falls between the field and the office.

Compliance

Subcontractor Compliance Tracking

Tracks certificates of insurance, licenses, lien waivers, and safety certifications for every sub on every project. Expiring documents trigger alerts. Work can be flagged before compliance issues become legal issues.

Bidding

Bid and Proposal Automation

Builds estimates from historical job cost data, current material pricing, and labor rates. Generates formatted proposals automatically. Tracks bid-to-win ratios by project type so you bid on the work that actually makes money.

Field

Field Data Capture and Progress Documentation

Crews log progress, photos, daily reports, and safety observations from their phones. Everything syncs to the project record in real time - timestamped, geotagged, and linked to the right phase and cost code.

Safety

Safety and Incident Reporting

Safety observations, near-misses, and incidents captured and categorized in the field. Patterns surface across sites and crews. OSHA-format reports generate automatically. Safety documentation doesn't live in a folder nobody checks.
