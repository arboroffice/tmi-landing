What TMI Builds

The entire construction operation, on one system.

Not six point tools bolted together. One operating system that runs every lane, from the first bid to collected cash, built for how construction actually runs.

Front office

Lead & Bid Intake

Every invitation to bid, RFP, and referral lands in one pipeline with the plans, addenda, and due dates attached. Nothing sits in an inbox until the deadline passes.

Front office

Estimating & Bids

Estimates build from historical cost codes, current material pricing, and real labor rates. Formatted proposals generate in hours, not days, and tie straight to the job budget if you win.

Front office

Bid Follow-Up

The system tracks every bid out the door and pings you on the ones gone quiet. Win rates get logged by project type, so you stop chasing work that never closes.

Field

Scheduling & Dispatch

Crews, equipment, and subs get scheduled against the project timeline on one board. RFIs and submittals route on the same system, so a stalled answer does not quietly push the whole job.

Field

Daily Logs & Photos

Crews log progress, daily reports, and jobsite photos from their phones. Everything syncs to the project record in real time, timestamped, geotagged, and tied to the right phase and cost code.

Field

Equipment & Materials

Track where equipment is, what it is running on, and which materials hit the site against what the estimate called for. Idle iron and material overages show up the day they happen.

Money

Progress Billing

AIA-style pay applications and progress invoices build from actual field progress, with retainage tracked on every line. Change orders flow in with pricing and owner approval before they hit the budget.

Money

Job Costing

Labor, materials, and equipment time post as they happen, not at month end. You see estimated against actual margin mid-job, and an alert fires when a phase runs over before it compounds.

Money

Lien Waivers & Collections

Conditional and unconditional lien waivers track against every payment. Aging invoices surface by job and by owner, so receivables stop sitting at ninety days while you fund the next mobilization.

Compliance

Safety & Certs

Near-misses and incidents get captured in the field and categorized for OSHA-format reporting. Certs and training records track to expiration, so a lapsed qualification never reaches a live jobsite.

Compliance

Subcontractor Management

Certificates of insurance, licenses, and compliance docs track for every sub on every project. Expiring paperwork triggers alerts and work gets flagged before a gap turns into a legal problem.

Command

One Dashboard

Every job, bid, crew, and dollar on one screen. The owner sees backlog, margin, cash position, and which jobs are slipping in real time, without waiting on a report someone has to assemble.
