How It Works

Five steps. One installed system.

  1. 01
    Audit

    We map your entire operation before touching a single tool. Where work comes in, where it stalls, where money leaks out. The audit is where the real work starts - and it's included.

  2. 02
    Blueprint

    We design the system architecture specific to your business. Which agents run what. How data flows between them. What your team touches versus what runs on its own. You see the full design before we build anything.

  3. 03
    Build

    We build it to your exact workflow - not a template. The agents, automations, and integrations are configured around the way your operation actually runs. No generic setups that require your team to change how they work to fit the software.

  4. 04
    Install

    We deploy the system and train your team on it. Not a video library. Hands-on onboarding until your people are running it without us. The install is done when your team says they're ready, not when we hit a deadline.

  5. 05
    Optimize

    Thirty days of post-install support is included. We watch the numbers, fix what the real world breaks, and tune the system once your team has actually been running it. Most of the best improvements come from this window.
