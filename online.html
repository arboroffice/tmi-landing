Questions

ANSWERED.

What exactly do you build, and does it connect to the tools I already use?
We build custom AI automations and workflows that plug into whatever tools you already use - Kajabi, Skool, GoHighLevel, Stripe, ConvertKit, Instagram, Gmail, Slack, and more. We do not force you to switch platforms. We wire up the ones you already have so they work together.
How is this different from hiring a VA or an OBM?
A VA does tasks. Infrastructure does tasks automatically, at any volume, at any hour, without sick days or turnover. Once it is built, the output is consistent and the cost does not scale with volume. A VA doing 10 hours of follow-up a week costs the same whether she books 2 calls or 20. Infrastructure costs the same either way and books the 20.
Do I need a big audience or a lot of existing content?
No. The lead and onboarding infrastructure works with whatever you already have. For living content builds, you need some existing content - a course, an ebook, a back catalogue. We work with what you have and build around it.
What does "yours forever" actually mean?
It means we do not hold the infrastructure hostage. We build it in your accounts, on your platforms, using your API keys. If you want to walk away from TMI after the handover, everything keeps running. You own it. No monthly access fee. No "we turn it off if you stop paying." It is yours.
Why only 6 builds per quarter?
Everything we build is fully custom. No templates. No copy-paste. We interview you, study your business, and build specifically for your voice, workflow, and audience. Six builds per quarter means every install gets full attention from a real team doing real work.
What if I have something specific that does not fit your packages?
A significant portion of what we build is custom one-off work. If you have a specific workflow, a specific problem, or an idea for something that should exist in your business - book a free strategy call and tell us about it. We will tell you whether we can build it and what it would cost.
