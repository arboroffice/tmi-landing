How it works

Four steps. Judge, refine, attention, grow.

Every app runs the same funnel. Most never make it past the first step, and that is the point.

01 Judge

Fresh eyes, hard questions.

We test how it actually works, read every word on the screen, and find the bugs you stopped seeing months ago. Then the questions that matter: does anyone truly need this, and does it make money the right way?

02 Refine

We fix it, not just flag it.

We rewrite the copy so it lands, pick the one feature that matters most and make it shine, and make sure the app feels native to the platform it lives on. It leaves sharper than it came in.

03 Attention

Now we make noise.

We run the Product Hunt launch, cut short videos for TikTok and Reels, and start real conversations with builders on X. We get the first wave of people to notice.

04 Grow

Systems that keep working after the noise fades.

Launch spikes die fast, so we build what compounds: affiliate programs so others sell it for you, app store optimization, small creators, and search traffic that keeps arriving long after launch week.
