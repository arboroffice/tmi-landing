The Problem

Where fleet operators lose time and margin

Dispatch decisions made on gut feel, compliance tracked on paper, and maintenance scheduled by guesswork — each one a source of cost that compounds across a fleet.

01

Dispatch is still a phone-and-whiteboard operation

Load assignments, driver availability, and route planning managed manually means idle trucks, missed pickups, and dispatchers stretched too thin.

02

DOT compliance is a paperwork burden

HOS logs, vehicle inspection reports, and driver qualification files tracked in binders and spreadsheets — one audit can expose significant gaps.

03

Maintenance is reactive, not preventive

Trucks go down when they break, not when you plan for it. Unscheduled breakdowns cost more than the repair — they cost loads, contracts, and driver time.
