Disconnect a service

You can disconnect any tool, bank, or account from TMI OS at any time. Disconnecting deletes the stored access and stops TMI from reading or acting on that service.

  1. Open TMI OS and sign in

    Go to the OS and sign in with the account that connected the service.

  2. Go to Connect tools

    In the left menu, open Connect tools (the Live data view). It lists every service connected to your company.

  3. Disconnect the service

    Find the connection and select Disconnect (or Remove for an MCP server). This immediately deletes the stored token and stops all syncing and actions. Only an owner can disconnect.

  4. Revoke on the provider too (recommended)

    For complete removal, also revoke TMI's access in the provider's own account settings (for example, the connected-apps or authorized-apps section of your accounting, bank, or CRM tool).

What happens when you disconnect

The stored credential is permanently deleted from TMI. Any metrics that service was feeding stop updating, and no worker can read from or act in that service. Disconnecting one service does not affect your other connections or your TMI OS account.

If you want your TMI OS account and all associated data deleted, email support@tmitechai.com and we will process the request as described in our Privacy Policy.
