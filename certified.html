About the Program

Not software training.
Operator training.

Every industry eventually develops a credential that separates operators who know what they're doing from those who guessed their way through. For AI in field operations, that credential is TMI Certified.

This isn't a platform certification for a specific tool. It's a recognition that someone understands how to run AI-powered operations - how to deploy it, manage it, measure it, and hold a team accountable to it.

Insurers, investors, and larger GCs are starting to ask whether your team is AI-capable. TMI Certified answers that question with documentation, not claims.

The program runs five days. Participants leave with a credential, a peer cohort, and a specific implementation plan for their operation.

TMI Certified is available for individual operators, management teams, and companies that want to certify their entire leadership bench. Each path has a different structure and price.

The first cohort runs in Q3 2026. Applications are open now.

Questions

Common questions.

When does the first cohort run?
The first TMI Certified cohort is scheduled for Q3 2026. Applications are open now. Cohort size is capped at 20 participants, and we expect to fill it from the waitlist. Apply early.
Is it in-person or remote?
The standard program is in-person, delivered at TMI Summit events or at a TMI facility. Remote cohorts are available for company certification and select individual seats. In-person cohorts produce better cohort relationships, and we recommend them when feasible.
Do I need to be using TMI software?
No. TMI Certified is platform-agnostic. The curriculum focuses on operational principles, deployment, and change management - not specific tools. Participants using TMI OS, ServiceTitan, Jobber, or their own systems are all welcome.
What if I don't pass the assessment?
Participants who don't pass the assessment on the first attempt get one remediation session and a second attempt within 90 days, included in the enrollment fee. We've designed the assessment to be rigorous, not arbitrary - everyone who completes the full five days in good faith has the foundation to pass.
How long does the credential last?
TMI Certified credentials are valid for two years from the date of issue. Renewal requires either re-certification or documented continued education through the TMI Network - at least four verified hours of coursework or peer learning per year.
Can I sponsor employees to attend?
Yes. Company-sponsored enrollment is the most common path for the Team and Company tiers. Invoicing is available to the company, and certificates are issued in the individual employee's name. Contact TMI directly for bulk enrollment invoicing.
