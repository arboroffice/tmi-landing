01 / Foundation

What TMI actually is

TMI is not software you sell a license to. We are an AI department and growth partner - growth operators who go into a business, delete the dead software and busywork, connect what is left, and build the intelligent backend that runs the operation. The client owns everything we build. It is a 30-day transformation, not a subscription.

Digital workers that own whole jobs end to end: the receptionist, the scheduler, the follow-up, the collector. No payroll, no training, never drop the ball.

03 / The pain you sell against

The problems they live with every day

You are not selling AI. You are selling the end of these specific, expensive leaks. Learn them cold, because your whole job is spotting which ones a prospect has.

04 / The product

What we build for them

Each of these is a digital employee or system that plugs a leak above. You do not need to know how they are built. You need to know what they do and which leak they fix.

AI Front Desk / Receptionist

Answers every call and message, day and night. Texts back every missed call in seconds, qualifies, and books straight into the calendar. Fixes: missed calls, slow follow-up, overwhelmed front desk.

Scheduling & No-Show Engine

Fills the calendar, confirms every appointment, and runs smart reminders so slots do not go empty. Fixes: no-shows, cancellations, manual scheduling.

Lead Response & Intake

Responds to every web lead and DM in under a minute, collects intake, and hands a ready patient to the desk. Fixes: slow follow-up, manual intake.

Reactivation Engine

Works the list of lapsed patients automatically and brings them back with the right message at the right time. Fixes: lapsed patients, a thin calendar.

Reviews & Reputation

Asks happy patients for a review at the perfect moment and routes unhappy ones to a private fix first. Fixes: reputation, new-patient flow.

Patient CRM & Retention

One record per patient, every visit and message tracked, follow-ups that fire on their own. Fixes: things slipping, retention.

Billing & AR Automation

Invoices, statements, and balance follow-up run on their own so earned money actually lands. Fixes: collections leaks.

The Command Center

The whole practice on one screen: schedule, new patients, revenue, and what needs attention, live, from a phone. Fixes: owner-as-bottleneck, no visibility.

On privacy: healthcare data is sensitive and clients will ask. Your line: "Everything is built with patient privacy and compliance in mind, and how we handle records is one of the first things we map on the call." Do not promise specifics or use the phrase "HIPAA-certified." Acknowledge it, then move to the booking. The build team owns the details.

08 / Handling pushback

Objection handling

Memorize the shape of each answer: acknowledge, reframe to the leak, move to the call. Never argue.

"It's too expensive."

"Compared to what? Most owners cover it from the missed patients we recover and the software we delete. That is exactly what the call figures out, and the call is free. Worth 30 minutes to see the number for your practice?"

"We already have software / a booking system."

"Good, we keep what works. Most practices do not have a software problem, they have a coordination problem: the tools do not talk, so things slip. We connect what you have and build only what is missing. Usually owners pay for less software after, not more."

"Is this a chatbot?"

"No. A chatbot answers when spoken to and stops. A digital employee owns the whole job: it answers the call, books the patient, confirms the appointment, and follows up on its own. It is a role on your team that does not take a salary."

"Will it replace my front desk staff?"

"No. It takes the repetitive part, the calls they miss and the follow-up they do not have time for, so your people do the human part: the patient in front of them. Your best staff stop drowning."

"What about patient privacy?"

"Fair question, and an important one. Everything is built with patient privacy and compliance in mind, and how we handle records is one of the first things we map on the call. The team walks you through exactly how it works for your setup."

"My front desk is fine."

"Might be. Quick test: call your own front desk at your busiest hour and see if a human answers. If it rings out even once a day, that is a patient a competitor just booked. The call shows you how many."

"Now is not a good time."

"Totally fair. The thing about a bottleneck is a busy season makes it louder, not quieter. The call is 30 minutes and free, and you walk away with the map either way. Want me to grab a time that is actually calm for you?"

"Send me some info first."

"Happy to, but honestly the info is your practice, not a brochure. The call is where you see where you specifically leak. Grab a time, and if it is not useful, you have lost 30 minutes. Text the line with anything before then: (337) 450-9795."

13 / Loose ends

FAQ

Do I need to know the tech to sell this?

No. You need to know the leaks (03), what we build to fix them (04), and the pricing (05). The build team owns the how. You own the problem and the booked call.

What if they ask something I don't know?

"Great question for the call, the team goes deep on that." Never make up an answer, especially on privacy or compliance. Booking the call is always the right next step.

How do I get paid / what's my target?

Your scorecard is booked discovery calls that turn into clients. Your manager sets your specific targets and comp; this guide is about making you good enough that the calls book themselves.

What makes healthcare & wellness different from other TMI verticals?

Higher patient lifetime value, so a missed call or a lapsed patient hurts more. Heavier front-desk and scheduling load. And privacy matters, so you acknowledge it and let the team handle specifics. The leaks are the same shape, they just cost more.
