11

The automation backbone

Recurring jobs (Vercel cron): rep-reminders daily 13:00 · visitor-digest Mon 13:00 · campaign-send weekdays 14:00 · webinar-cron every 10 min · visitor-process every 6h.

Delayed steps (Upstash QStash): all "+N hours/days" nurture — audit-nudge, audit-followup, funnel-nurture, followup, webinar-nurture — are scheduled at the triggering event and fired later by QStash, secret-gated with GTM_RUN_SECRET.

Webhooks: Cal.com (booking created/ended/cancelled/no-show) · Stripe (paid / deposit / refund) · RB2B (hot visitor) · Instantly (cold reply) · Twilio (inbound SMS) · inbound email.

Every send is logged to the Account 360 timeline via _comms.js (SMS → sms_log, email → activities). All sends are best-effort and never block the request.
TMI internal reference · generated from a full codebase audit of /api. Prospect nurture stops automatically on conversion or unsubscribe/STOP. Founder-only forms (Make Money With AI, Start a Business, Distro) intentionally send no applicant confirmation.
