Fixes: missed calls, lost inquiries, after-hours dead air

Digital employee: front desk and intake

A digital employee is a system that owns a defined piece of recurring work from beginning to end, has a human owner, and can be held to a standard. At the front desk, it answers inbound calls and messages, captures the details, books the appointment onto the calendar, and routes anything urgent to a person. It assigns the day's jobs by skill, location, priority and drive time, keeps the board current as things move, and fills holes when a job cancels. After: the board is a shared record, and the dispatcher supervises it instead of rebuilding it. It builds a first estimate off the company's own pricing and job history, gets it out while the customer is still interested, and follows up until the customer answers or says no.
