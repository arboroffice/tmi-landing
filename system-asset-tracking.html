What It Is

Every asset accounted for, at every moment.

Equipment goes missing. Tools get double-booked. Vehicles sit idle because nobody knows they're available. Asset tracking solves the awareness problem - every piece of equipment, every tool, every vehicle gets a location, a status, and a history that anyone in the operation can access in real time without making a phone call.

TMI's asset tracking infrastructure integrates with your dispatch, maintenance, and billing systems so asset data isn't siloed. When a machine is assigned to a job, maintenance history follows. When it's due for service, the scheduler knows. When it comes back, utilization is logged automatically. The result is a complete operational picture of everything you own or operate.

How It Works

From location to utilization to maintenance - automatically.

01

Real-time location and status

Every tagged asset - tool, machine, or vehicle - reports its location and status continuously. Field teams see availability without calling the yard. Dispatchers assign without guessing what's out.

02

Utilization and assignment tracking

Every check-out, job assignment, and return is logged automatically. Utilization rates surface by asset, by crew, by site. You see what's overworked and what's sitting idle before budgeting the next rental.

03

Maintenance history attached

Service records, inspection results, and work order history travel with every asset. When a machine moves sites, its entire history moves with it. No rebuilding records, no "who last touched this" conversations.

Who Benefits

Any operation moving equipment across sites.

Asset tracking pays off fastest for companies running multiple sites, large equipment fleets, or high-value tool inventories.

Construction

Tools and equipment move between sites constantly. Tracking eliminates the daily scramble to locate gear, prevents double-booking, and shows exactly what's on which site before the foreman drives out to check.

Oil & Gas

High-value equipment spread across remote locations. Asset tracking gives operations managers a single view of equipment status, maintenance due dates, and location - without radio calls to site supervisors.

Heavy Equipment

Utilization rates, idle time, and job assignments tracked automatically across every machine in the fleet. Rental decisions backed by data, not gut feel. Equipment downtime caught before it kills a job schedule.

Utilities

Specialized tools and vehicles tracked across service territories. Crew dispatchers see real availability before assigning. Equipment history ensures compliance requirements travel with the asset.

Field Services

Van inventory, specialty tools, and shared equipment tracked in real time. Parts and tools reach the right technician before the call, not after. No more service delays because the right equipment is on the wrong truck.
