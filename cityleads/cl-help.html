Frequently Asked Questions
How do I log a visit?

Go to Quick Capture from the bottom nav or the "Capture" tab. Select the business you visited, choose the visit type (Walk-In, Call, etc.), add what happened, and hit Log Visit. The visit will appear in your Visits history and update the business timeline automatically.

How do I hand off a lead?

Open the business profile and tap "Handoff to Sales." Fill in what you know about the owner, what problem they have, and what you've already discussed. Tyler gets notified immediately and will pick it up from there. You can track the handoff status in the Handoffs section.

When does my commission get paid?

Commission is paid on the 1st of the month following the month a deal goes live (not just closed — fully onboarded). Referral bonus for deals your contacts bring in pays at the same time. You can see all pending and paid amounts in My Earnings.

How do I change my assigned city?

City assignments are managed by the admin team. Book office hours with Mia to discuss a city change request. In most cases it takes 2–4 weeks to transition depending on pipeline status in your current city.

What counts as a qualifying visit?

Any in-person contact at the business location. Phone calls and emails count as follow-up activity but not as visits. The business must have 3 or more employees and operate in the trades, construction, or field services. Log it the same day — retroactive logging is flagged for review.
