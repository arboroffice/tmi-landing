What TMI Builds

The entire mining operation, on one system.

Not six point tools bolted together. One operating system that runs every lane, from the production plan to collected cash per ton, built for how mining actually runs.

Front office

Production Planning

The mine plan, daily targets, and pit sequence live in one place against actual capacity. Planners set tonnage and grade goals by area, and the plan updates as conditions and equipment availability change.

Front office

Cost-Per-Ton Planning

Cost models build from fuel, labor, equipment hours, and consumables against planned tonnage. You see the cost per ton a plan implies before the shift runs, not after the month closes and the numbers slip.

Front office

Contractor Bids

Drill, blast, and haulage contractor scopes and bids track in one pipeline with rates and terms attached. Awarded work ties straight to the production plan and the cost model, so nothing runs off a side spreadsheet.

Field

Dispatch & Haul Cycles

Haul trucks, loaders, and shovels get dispatched against the plan, with cycle times tracked live. A slow haul cycle shows up as equipment, road condition, or operator pattern, so supervisors fix the bottleneck on shift.

Field

Shift Logs & Production

Operators log tonnage, grade, and downtime from the cab. Structured handoff reports generate at end of shift, pulling equipment status, production totals, and open items so a 24-hour operation never runs on verbal briefings.

Field

Fuel & Consumables

Fuel, tires, GET, and consumables track by asset against production. Burn rates surface by truck and fleet, and reorder triggers fire on lead time so a remote site never runs dry mid-cycle.

Money

Invoicing Per Ton

Invoices and royalty statements build off measured tonnage and grade, tied to the shipment record. Production reconciles as material leaves the site, not in a month-end reconciliation against the weighbridge.

Money

Cost Per Ton

Fuel, labor, equipment hours, and consumables post against actual tonnage as the shift runs. You see cost per ton against plan in real time, and an alert fires when an area drifts over before the quarter is lost.

Money

Collections & AR

Aging invoices surface by customer and by shipment. Receivables stop sitting while you fund the next campaign, and slow payers get flagged before you load out another trainload.

Compliance

MSHA Safety & Certs

MSHA certifications, training records, and equipment inspection completion track automatically. Incidents get digitized for regulatory submission, and safety trends surface by shift and crew before they become a reportable event.

Compliance

Predictive Maintenance

Failure models run against your haul trucks, draglines, crushers, and drills using vibration, oil analysis, and load-cycle data. A failure caught 72 hours early costs a fraction of an unplanned shutdown, and parts get staged before the machine goes down.

Command

One Dashboard

Every pit, fleet, shift, and dollar on one screen. The site sees tonnage, grade, cost per ton, equipment health, and cash position in real time, with critical alerts reaching supervisors by SMS fallback when connectivity drops.
