Free Entry Point
Operations Audit
You map your operation on a single call. We find where the time and money are leaking and show you exactly what TMI can fix. No pitch. No pressure. Just clarity. This is the door into everything else. Nothing gets built until the audit is done.
Entry Build
TMI Zero
The foundational AI back office setup. Call agent, quote agent, basic automation. Built for operators who are ready to stop running everything manually but want to see the system working before committing to the full build. Most Zero clients move to the AI Wing Build within 60 to 90 days.
Flagship Build
The AI Wing Build
The full custom AI infrastructure build. Custom agents, integrations, and full deployment built specifically around your business. This is the main product. Automation, lead follow-up, client onboarding, revenue reporting, dispatch, invoicing, content systems - the whole stack. Scoped to your complexity and installed by the team that built it.
Specific Problem
One-Off Tool Build
A single tool or agent built for a single leak. Not the full system. One specific thing that solves one specific problem. Best for operators who have a particular bottleneck they want fixed without a full infrastructure engagement.
Optional
AI Department
The fractional AI department retainer. TMI stays in, maintains the systems, builds new ones as the operation grows, and keeps the infrastructure ahead of the business. Most clients who complete a build stay on retainer. The system keeps compounding.
Executive Level
Chief AI Officer Engagement
Private one-on-one at the leadership level. For larger operations that need TMI embedded at the strategy layer, not just the operational layer. Custom architecture, built to spec. Everything built is owned completely by the client.
Licensing
IP Licensing
TMI's frameworks, workflows, and agent systems licensed to other operators or organizations. For operators who want to run TMI's infrastructure internally, or organizations that want to deploy it across a network of businesses they work with.
Trainings / Events
Operator & Team Training
Operator trainings and team trainings run as standalone engagements. We teach tech-native workers how to use AI tools to speed up their day-to-day work - not theory, hands-on. Built for field operators, office teams, and anyone in between. Can run as a one-time session or a structured program.
