How we work

Step by step. Every time.

Every TMI engagement follows the same sequence. You know exactly what happens next — and so does your team.

01
Audit Call
One call to map the operation. We find where the time and money are leaking before we propose anything. This is the door into every engagement — nothing gets built until the audit is done.
02
Business Mapping
We document the full operation — workflows, tools, team structure, data sources, and the three to five highest-leverage places to build. This becomes the blueprint everything else is built from.
03
Workflow Selection
We agree on what gets built first. Prioritized by ROI, implementation speed, and what the team is ready to absorb. Scope is fixed before build begins. No scope creep, no surprises.
04
System Build
We build. Custom agents, automations, integrations — all of it wired into the tools and workflows your operation already runs. Built for the way your team actually works, not for how a product manager imagines they do.
05
Testing
Every system is tested against real operational scenarios before it goes live. Edge cases, failure modes, the things that break at 6am on a Tuesday — we find them before your team does.
06
Team Training
We train the people who will run it. Hands-on, not slides. Field teams, office teams, managers — whoever needs to know the system learns it before launch. Adoption is part of the build.
07
Launch
The system goes live with TMI alongside it. We are in the operation during launch — monitoring, adjusting, and making sure the first week runs clean. No handing over documentation and disappearing.
08
30-Day Optimization
The first thirty days after launch are where the system gets dialed in. Real usage surfaces things staging never does. We tune, adjust, and improve until the system runs the way the operation needs it to.
09
Monthly Support
Most clients stay on retainer. The system keeps running, TMI keeps improving it, and every new build compresses the time and cost of the next one. The infrastructure compounds. That is the point.

This is not a project that ends at delivery. Every build is the foundation for the next one.
