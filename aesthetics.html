What's costing you

Three problems every aesthetics business deals with.

01
Leads cool off before anyone replies
Inquiries come from social, forms, and missed calls. The client books wherever answers first. Speed to lead decides the sale, and it runs through one busy front desk.
02
No-shows leave empty chairs
A missed appointment is revenue you never get back. Reminders are inconsistent, deposits are hit or miss, and open slots rarely get refilled before they vanish.
03
Clients come once and disappear
Great treatment, then silence. No follow-up, no membership offer, no nudge when it is time to come back. The lifetime value walks right out the door.
