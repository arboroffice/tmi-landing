What TMI Builds

Six systems. Built for electrical operations.

Custom infrastructure that connects licensing, permits, job costs, and billing into one operating layer - without the manual overhead.

Dispatch

Certification-Based Dispatch

Routes every job to a tech with the right license. Master electrician requirements, commercial certifications, specialty permits - the system reads the job spec and matches automatically. Certification expiration dates tracked and flagged before they become your liability.

Compliance

Permit Tracking and Application Management

Connects to municipal portals and tracks every permit from application to final inspection. Jobs with pending approvals are held from scheduling automatically. Inspection delays are logged so you know exactly where the bottleneck is - not three weeks later when the GC calls.

Finance

Commercial Job Costing

Tracks estimated versus actual in real time. As materials are pulled, hours logged, and change orders approved, the job margin updates automatically. No end-of-project surprise. No reconciliation meeting. You see where you stand on every commercial job the moment it changes.

Billing

Billing and Progress Payment Automation

Generates progress invoices on schedule, captures every change order in a signed document trail, and pushes completed job invoices to your accounting system without manual entry. Nothing leaves the site undocumented. Every billable hour and material is captured before the crew moves on.

Safety

Compliance and Safety Reporting

OSHA certifications, safety training records, PPE logs, and incident reports - all tracked and reported from the field. Crews complete digital safety forms on mobile. Compliance dashboards show what is current, expiring, and overdue without anyone managing a spreadsheet of dates.

CRM

Service Agreement and Maintenance CRM

Manages recurring maintenance contracts, service agreement renewals, and customer communication automatically. Renewal notices go out on schedule. Service histories are attached to every account. Your recurring revenue base gets managed without manual follow-up from your office staff.

TMI builds a dispatch layer that reads the job requirements - panel upgrades require a master electrician, certain commercial work requires specific certifications - and automatically routes to a qualified tech. It also tracks certification expiration dates and flags upcoming renewals so a tech never shows up to a job they are not licensed for. If a certification lapses, that tech gets removed from eligible dispatch for that job type until the renewal is confirmed.

TMI connects to municipal permit portals and tracks every permit application from submission to approval to final inspection. Jobs with pending permits are automatically flagged so schedulers do not book work before approvals come through. Inspection delays are logged and reported so you know exactly where the bottlenecks are - not when the general contractor calls to ask why you have not pulled the permit yet.
