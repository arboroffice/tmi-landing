How we think about ownership

We buy companies to build them, not strip them.

We improve operations, expand margins, strengthen leadership, and increase enterprise value. Most capital arrives, cuts costs, and waits for a multiple to move. We work the other direction.

We do not buy companies to cut costs. We build them into stronger, smarter, more valuable assets, so the return comes from a better operating business, not from a spreadsheet.

The company gets better. Then the asset compounds. In that order.

Our investment model

Capital shaped to fit the company.

The structure follows the situation. We engage at the level of ownership that lets us improve the operating business and align on the long term.

01

Strategic partnerships

We bring systems, intelligence, and operational depth to a company we do not need to own to improve.

02

Revenue-share agreements

We install the transformation and are paid from the upside we create, with incentives aligned to results.

03

Minority investments

We take a stake and a seat close to the operating decisions, backing founders who want to build the larger version.

04

Growth investment

We back proven operators with the capital and systems to grow faster than they could alone.

05

Long-term partnership

We stay involved for the long term, aligned on building the larger, more valuable version of the company.

06

Aligned for the long term

No forced exit clock. We are aligned on building value that compounds for years, not quarters.

The path

From first conversation to compounding asset.

A relationship with TMI Capital can start anywhere on this path and move forward as trust and results grow. Each stage makes the next one stronger.

Stage 01

Consulting

We assess the human and the company, find the real constraints, and prove where value is being lost.

Stage 02

Technology

We install the operating systems, AI employees, and business intelligence that make the company legible and fast.

Stage 03

Partnership

We move from vendor to partner, with shared goals and a stake in the outcome we are building together.

Stage 04

Investment

We put capital behind the transformation, taking a minority position to fund the next stage of growth.

Stage 05

Long-term growth

We stay in it for the long term, aligned on building a stronger, smarter, less owner-dependent company that is worth more every year.
