Questions

Before you start.

Do I have to buy every layer?

No. You start with the one layer that fixes the most expensive problem, usually the core operating system or one digital employee. Every layer you add later plugs into the same system, so you grow it at your pace.

How is this different from buying software?

Software gives everyone the same product and leaves you to integrate it. TMI builds one system around your operation, your workflows, and your terminology, and every layer shares the same data and command center. You own it instead of renting it.

Can I start with just one layer?

Yes. Most owners start with one layer, see what it takes off their plate, and stack the rest over time. The audit tells you which layer to install first.

What does it cost?

Every engagement starts with the Intelligent Company Audit. From there, scope and price depend on the layers you install. There is no monthly software license, and most owners recover the cost from the software they cut and the time they get back.

How long does it take?

The core build is installed in about 30 days. Additional layers are added on top of the system that is already running, so each one goes live faster than the last.

Do I own what you build?

Yes. You own every layer we build, the data it holds, and the decisions it makes. No monthly license and no lock-in.
