The math nobody says out loud

Your employees are paid to do a job. Not to grow your revenue.

Payroll is your biggest cost, and it is fixed. Every hire is a bet that more hands means more growth, but a salary is not tied to revenue. People are paid to show up and clear the work in front of them, not to make the company bigger. So the operation gets heavier, overhead climbs, and growth still rests on you.

A salary never moves with your revenue. Ours can.

How the partnership works

We embed as your growth team and get paid on what we build.

Instead of a big monthly retainer, we tie our compensation to the value we help create. If the company does not grow, we do not expect to earn much. If it doubles, triples, or becomes the leader in its market, we share in what we helped build. Think less agency, more private-equity operating partner.

One goal: increase revenue, increase profit, increase company value. As the company grows, everyone wins.

How we get paid, tied to what we build

Revenue share

A share of the new revenue we help generate, stepping up as the company scales.

Profit share

A share of the increase in profit over a baseline, so we are paid on what actually reaches the bottom line.

Equity + revenue share

Founder friendly: a small stake that vests over time, plus a share of revenue while we actively drive growth.

Milestone equity

Equity that unlocks as you hit revenue milestones, so everyone stays pointed at the same targets.

Add performance bonuses that vest automatically when we hit the milestones that matter: a new location, a new service line, an acquisition, an EBITDA target. We do not want clients. We want partners, tied to the value we help you build.
