Questions

Common questions.

What happens after 30 days?
You own the system and run it yourself. If you want to add more systems, you can engage for a full TMI OS deployment. If you want peer support and resources, you can continue your TMI Network membership at $297/month. Neither is required.
What software are we installing?
TMI selects and configures the right tool for your operation — either from our Marketplace or TMI's own OS modules. We don't push a specific platform. We configure what fits your work and your crew.
Do I need any technical staff?
No. TMI runs the entire install. You need someone who can coordinate training schedules and has access to your company data — that's it.
Can we do more than one system?
Start Kit installs one system. If you need a full multi-system deployment, that's a TMI OS engagement — a different scope, different timeline, different price. We recommend starting with one unless you've already operated with digital systems before.
What if the install goes sideways?
It's why we have a four-week timeline instead of two. The pilot week exists to find problems before the whole crew is on the system. We've done this enough to know where things break, and we build the fix into the process.
Is there a revenue share?
Start Kit has no revenue share. It's a flat fee. Revenue share is part of Operator-tier TMI Network membership — a different, deeper engagement for companies that want an ongoing operational partnership.
