What we built

Home service · Sales + ops OS

HomePro: instant estimates and a backend that runs the business

The problem

A home-service business was losing jobs to slow quotes and losing track of everything after the sale, leads, follow-ups, financing objections, cash, and which customers were quietly heading toward a system failure.

What we built

Instant estimates that price a job on the spot, plus a complete backend OS. AI answers and qualifies every lead in seconds, builds the quote, and chases follow-ups, while the owner dashboard reads the business like a doctor reads vitals, close rate, cash runway, retention, and revenue in one live view.
