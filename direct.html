TMI Direct

Invite
only.

There is no application form for TMI Direct. There is no pricing page. Invitations are extended by TMI to companies we're already working with closely.

This page exists to explain what it is, who gets invited, and how you get there.

What It Is

The deepest
TMI relationship.

TMI Direct is a white-glove operational partnership. Not a consulting retainer. Not a managed service. A genuine co-build relationship where TMI and a Direct partner are working toward the same outcome — the partner's operational transformation and, in some cases, a new product or market together.

Direct partners have a different kind of access. Not just to tools and the network, but to TMI leadership, TMI engineers, and in some cases TMI capital. The relationship is structured differently for every Direct partner, because no two are in the same situation.

Most TMI Network members never become Direct partners. That's not a failure — the Operator tier is already a significant operational partnership and most companies get everything they need from it.

Direct is for companies where TMI has decided the relationship is worth more than a monthly fee structure can capture. Where the upside is big enough that both sides want skin in the game.

What's Included

No standard
package.

Every Direct partnership is structured differently. The following are the kinds of things Direct partnerships include — not a checklist that every partner gets.

Direct TMI leadership access
A standing relationship with TMI founders and senior team. Not account management — direct access to the people making product and strategy decisions. Used when the partner needs a decision, not a process.
Co-build engagements
TMI engineers embedded in the partner's operational build. Building custom agents, integrations, or systems that aren't on the standard platform roadmap. Structured as a co-build with shared IP or as a funded development engagement.
TMI Funding access
Direct partners have priority access to TMI Funding structures — equipment financing, working capital, and in some cases TMI direct investment. The operational relationship informs the capital relationship.
Equity structures
For some Direct partnerships, TMI takes a small equity position in exchange for sustained engagement, product development, or capital contribution. These are negotiated individually and not offered to all Direct partners.
Market development
TMI introduces Direct partners into its network as the preferred operator in their market and trade. This isn't a referral program — it's TMI actively positioning the partner as who we trust in their space.
Ghost Crew deployment priority
Direct partners have first access to Ghost Crew deployments — on-demand robotic equipment and AI-staffed operational capacity for projects that need it. Deployed on a custom schedule, not through the standard marketplace.
Who Gets Invited

The signals
TMI looks for.

Direct invitations aren't awarded for revenue or company size. They're extended when TMI sees a combination of operational ambition and mutual upside.

Already an Operator member

Every Direct partner has been through the Operator tier first. It's how both sides learn whether the relationship can go deeper. TMI doesn't invite strangers to Direct.

Building something significant

A company making a major operational bet — a new market, a new service model, a technology deployment that will define their position for the next five years. Not a company maintaining the status quo.

Aligned incentives

The partner wants what TMI is building to succeed. They're deploying it, teaching their crews, and betting their operations on it — not evaluating it as a vendor option.

Mutual upside

There's a reason for TMI to invest beyond the monthly fee. Either the partner's market is a new one TMI wants to enter, the partner's scale makes the relationship worth more, or there's a product to build together.

Long time horizon

Direct partnerships are multi-year relationships. Companies that want a quick result or are planning an exit in 12 months are not the right fit. The relationship compounds over time.

Proven leadership

The people running the company are operators first. They make fast decisions, hold their teams accountable, and don't need TMI to manage their business — they need us to help them build it faster.

The Path

How you
get there.

If you're reading this and thinking about how to position yourself for a Direct invitation, here's what that path actually looks like.

Step 01

Join the Network

Start at Member or apply for Operator. The network is where TMI gets to know you — how you think, what you're building, and whether there's more to the relationship.

Step 02

Operate at Operator tier

The Operator tier is a real partnership with real accountability. The monthly ops review, the account lead, the revenue structure — it's the proving ground for Direct candidates.

Step 03

Build something real

Deploy TMI systems in your operation. Use the OS. Run Ghost Crew pilots. Deploy agents in your field. Show that you're not evaluating — you're building.

Step 04

TMI extends the invitation

When there's mutual upside and a relationship that's proven itself, TMI reaches out. Not the other way around. There's no nomination process or application — it's a conversation that starts organically.

There's no form to fill out.

If you're asking how to apply for TMI Direct, the answer is: become an Operator member and build something worth noticing. The invitation comes from the work, not the ask.
