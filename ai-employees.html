Questions

Things people ask before they start.

Is this just software I have to run?

No. A digital employee is built and managed by us. We set it up around your business, connect it to your tools, and keep it running. You are hiring a worker, not buying another app to figure out.

Do I have to start with all of them?

No, start with one. Pick the single most painful, repetitive job and hand it over. Once you see what it takes off your plate, you can add more. Most owners stack them over time.

What if I need something not on the list?

We build custom ones. The roster is the most common roles, but if you have a specific repetitive task, tell us and we will scope a digital employee for it.

How is this different from the full TMI build?

This is the easy way in. The full intelligent-company build rebuilds your whole backend. TMI Workforce lets you start with one role, one outcome, right now. Many owners begin here and grow into the full build.

How fast can one be working?

About 24 hours. Once we understand the job and connect your tools, the employee is set up and working. No long onboarding.

What does it cost?

One simple monthly price per employee, scoped to the role. No setup fees and no per-task billing. Bundle a team and it works out lower per role. Book a call and we will price the exact role for your business.

Is there a contract?

No long lock-in. Add, pause, or cancel any time. The work it does is what keeps you, not a contract.
