Questions

Things people ask before they apply.

Who are the programs for?

Companies, founders, leaders, teams, and individuals. Some owners want 1:1 coaching, some want their whole team trained, some want a room of peers. We scope the right format for who it is for.

How is this different from the done-for-you build?

This is the learn-it-and-own-it path. The build hands you finished systems. The programs teach you and your team to lead, run, and improve them. Many people do both, learn first, then have us build.

What does $10k get me?

It is the starting point. Final pricing depends on the program, the format, and how many people are involved. We scope it with you after we understand the outcome you want.

Are masterminds in person or virtual?

Both, depending on the room. Some are local and in person, some run virtually. We will tell you what is forming and where when you apply.

Can you train my employees specifically?

Yes. Team AI training is built around how your company actually works, so your people leave able to use AI and the systems day to day, not in theory.

What happens after I apply?

We talk. We learn who it is for and what you want to get out of it, then recommend the right program and price.
