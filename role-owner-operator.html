The Problem

Running the operation and running the business are two different jobs.

Most owner-operators are doing both at once, which means neither gets the attention it deserves. The business grows until the owner becomes the ceiling. Hiring adds headcount but not capacity - because the work that matters still runs through you.

TMI doesn't add software to manage. It builds the operational layer underneath your business so the right things happen without you in the middle. Work gets assigned. Invoices go out. Jobs close without a phone call to you.

When something actually needs your attention, you find out. Everything else handles itself.

How It Works

Built to run without you.

01

Assignment without you

AI assigns the right people, routes work, and handles exceptions in real time. When something runs late or a job changes, the system re-evaluates and adjusts - without a call to you. You're not the decision layer for every move.

02

Work to invoice, automatically

Job data captured as the work happens flows straight to billing. Invoices go out within hours of close. The system tracks payment and flags anything overdue - without you managing a spreadsheet or chasing a client.

03

Alerts only for what matters

You get notified when something genuinely needs your judgment - a job that's at risk, a client escalating, a cost overrun. Not every status update. The system filters the noise so your attention goes where it's actually needed.
