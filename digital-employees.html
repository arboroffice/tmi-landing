What a digital employee actually is

A real role on a cadence, not a box you have to prompt.

A chatbot sits and waits. You have to know it exists, open it, and type the right question to get anything out of it. The work only happens when you remember to ask. Most of the work in a company never gets asked.

A digital employee is the opposite. It owns a job. It knows what to do at 6am and at 11pm without anyone opening an app. It reads your data, does the task, writes the result back into your systems, and tells you what it did. When something is outside its lane, it stops and asks.

A chatbot waits to be prompted. A digital employee runs its role on a schedule you set.
A chatbot lives in one window. A digital employee works inside your CRM, your inbox, and your job board.
A chatbot answers a question. A digital employee finishes a task and logs it.
A chatbot forgets the last conversation. A digital employee is grounded in your company's own knowledge every time.

You are not buying a smarter chatbot. You are hiring a worker that shows up.

Example roles

The jobs a digital employee can hold.

Each one owns a specific role and runs it on a cadence. Start with one, add more as they prove out. Anything that reaches a customer or moves money waits for your approval.

Dispatcher

Assigns and confirms jobs.

Matches the right crew to the right job, sends the assignment, and confirms it landed. When a crew goes quiet or a slot opens up, it flags it before the day falls apart.

Collections

Chases overdue invoices.

Watches every invoice past its terms and works the follow-up sequence so money stops sitting in accounts receivable. The message to the customer waits for your approval before it goes out.

Follow-up

Never lets a lead go cold.

Every quote, every callback, every "let me think about it" gets worked on a schedule instead of dying in someone's inbox. The lead that would have been forgotten gets touched on time.

Reporting

Numbers ready every morning.

Pulls the day's revenue, jobs, aging, and open work from your systems and has it waiting before you finish your coffee. No one has to build the report. It is already done.

Intake

Captures and routes new work.

Takes every new lead and job that comes in, captures the details, and routes it to the right place so nothing gets lost between the call and the calendar.

Scheduling

Keeps the calendar honest.

Books, reschedules, and confirms appointments so the day matches reality. When two jobs collide or a window opens, it surfaces the conflict instead of letting it show up on site.

How they work

Built to run your work, not your day.

A digital employee is not a magic trick. It is a worker with a clear job, clear boundaries, and a clear line back to you. Here is how it operates.

01

Runs on a cadence

It works its role on a schedule you set, morning and night, without anyone opening an app or remembering to ask.

02

Grounded in your knowledge

It reads from your company's own data, pricing, and process, so it acts like it works here, not like a generic bot.

03

Escalates on its own

When something is outside its lane or looks wrong, it stops and hands it to a human instead of guessing.

04

Asks for approval

Anything that contacts a customer or moves money waits for your sign-off until you decide it can run on its own.

05

You stay in control

Every action is logged and reversible. You set the boundaries, and you can widen or tighten them any time.
