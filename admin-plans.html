What happens every month to keep every client
Monthly Touchpoints
Week 1
Monthly Audit
Re-run the operational audit. Review each category score against prior month. Flag any new dependencies or friction points that have emerged.
Delivery
Week 1
System Updates
Push any pending improvements to their AI workflows. Fix errors from the prior month. Apply updates to integrations, prompts, or automations.
Delivery
Week 2
New Workflow Added
Every month the client gets one new system built. It could be a dispatcher tool, an invoice follow-up sequence, a job costing report, or a new crew-facing workflow.
Delivery
Week 3
Performance Report
Send a concise report: what systems ran, how many automations fired, where time was saved, what problems were caught. One page. Numbers only, no fluff.
Delivery
Week 4
Owner Check-In
20-minute call with the owner. Walk through what changed this month. Ask about any new problems. This is the relationship call, not a status update. Keep it short and direct.
Account
Ongoing
AI Error Review
Weekly scan for failed automations, hallucinated outputs, or edge cases the system mishandled. Fix silently where possible. Flag anything that affected the client's operation.
Delivery
Ongoing
Opportunity Map
Maintain a live list of the next 3-5 things we could build for this client. Use it to drive the owner check-in. When the map is full, the client is always expanding, never churning.
