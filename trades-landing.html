What's actually happening

The field runs fine. It's everything else that's killing your margin.

The work gets done. The problem is the 40 hours a week of admin, follow-up, scheduling, invoicing, and reporting that happens around the work.

01
Dispatch is still a phone call and a whiteboard
Someone calls in sick. Two techs show up to the same job. A customer waits four hours past their window. You find out at the end of the day when the complaints come in.
02
Estimates go out slow and come back wrong
Your best estimator is busy on the job site. New leads sit for three days. When the estimate finally goes out, materials are priced from memory. You win the job and lose money on it.
03
Change orders get missed, invoices go out late
Extra work gets done. Nobody writes it down. The invoice goes out for the original scope. You completed $12k of work and billed $9k. That's not a cash flow problem - that's a systems problem.
04
Your best tech just became your biggest single point of failure
He knows the system. He knows every customer. He knows the parts room. He's been here 11 years. He's also 54 years old. When he leaves, half your operation leaves with him.
05
You can't see the business. You can only feel it.
Is the Riverside crew actually profitable? Which job type has the worst margin? What's your cost per lead by channel? You don't know because the data lives in three spreadsheets and nobody's head.
