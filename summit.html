What It's Like

Not a conference.
A working session.

TMI Summit is designed around the things people actually get value from at conferences — the hallway conversations, the dinners, the sessions where someone says the thing that isn't on the slides. We build the event around those moments.

Fifty operators max

Hard cap at 50. We've been to conferences with 2,000 people and left knowing none of them. Every person at TMI Summit is someone you can actually have a conversation with.

No vendor booths

Sponsors get a mention, not a booth. There's no expo hall. The people in the room are operators, not salespeople. If a vendor wants to attend, they come as an operator — not as a sponsor.

Operated sessions

Most sessions are run by operators, not speakers. A company walks through what they built, what broke, what it cost, and what it's worth now. Q&A is the main event.

Roundtable format

Afternoon sessions run as roundtables by trade — construction, trades, oil & gas, fleet. Small groups, same problems. The conversation gets specific fast.

Live builds

TMI runs a live build session each day — picking an operational problem from the room and working through a real solution in real time. You watch it get built, not just described.

Post-summit network

Everyone who attends gets 30-day Member access to the TMI Network after the event. The relationships from the room continue in the peer community.

Sample Agenda

Two days.
How it runs.

Day One
8:30 AM

Doors open — coffee and introductions

Structured networking with company + problem prompts. Everyone says what they're actually working on.

9:30 AM

State of the Field — TMI

What's changed in the last 12 months. What's working, what isn't, and what's coming. 30 minutes, no slides.

10:15 AM

Operator Build #1 — Full company walkthrough

A featured operator presents their AI deployment end to end. Timeline, cost, what broke, current numbers. 45 min + Q&A.

12:00 PM

Working lunch — Trade roundtables

Grouped by industry. Structured conversation with a facilitator. No presentations.

2:00 PM

Live Build — TMI team

TMI takes a real operational problem submitted by attendees and builds a solution live. You watch the agents get configured in real time.

4:00 PM

Operator Build #2 — Shorter format

Two operators, 20 minutes each. One thing they built, one thing that surprised them.

6:30 PM

Dinner — TMI hosted

Included for all attendees. Seated dinner with open seating. Usually runs late.

Day Two
9:00 AM

Operator Build #3

Featured operator with a specific failure story — what they tried, what it cost, what they'd do differently.

10:30 AM

TMI Platform Preview

Upcoming systems, agents in development, and what's coming in the next six months. Attendees get early access.

12:00 PM

Working lunch — Problem clinics

Attendees submit specific problems. Small groups of 4–6 work through each one with a TMI facilitator.

2:00 PM

Live Build #2

Second live build session based on morning problem clinic submissions.

4:00 PM

Close + next steps

Commitments from the room. Everyone says one thing they're going to implement in the next 30 days. Network setup for attendees.
