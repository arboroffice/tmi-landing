In practice
03

The specific work we automate first.

The highest-value AI applications in oil and gas and the trades are not theoretical. They are the same workflows your teams do every day, manually. Here is where we start.

Field Operations

Field data, captured at the source.

Handwritten tickets, voice memos, photos, structured and synced to your ERP before the crew drives back. No duplicate entry. No cleanup. No two sources of truth.

Asset Management

Maintenance ranked by what's actually at risk.

Sensor alerts, work orders, and technician notes combined into one prioritised list of what to fix next. Not every alert is urgent. This one knows the difference.

Compliance

Compliance reports that write themselves.

Environmental, safety, and operational filings drafted from the data you're already generating. Missing fields flagged. Audit trails built. Deadline drama eliminated.

Safety

Safety inspections that don't end at the form.

Field photos and notes turned into corrective-action tasks, routed to the right person, tracked to close. Near-miss reporting in thirty seconds. Patterns surface before incidents do.

Upstream

Anomalies caught before they become downtime.

Production, pressure, flow, and downtime patterns watched continuously. Not another dashboard. An attention system that acts before small deviations become expensive problems.

Knowledge

Every engineering doc, instantly searchable.

SOPs, well logs, simulation outputs, lessons learned, searchable in plain language. Ask the question; get the answer with the source. Institutional knowledge stops walking out the door.

Emissions

Emissions data, closed-loop.

Sensor readings, manual entries, and field logs consolidated, gaps flagged, and filings pre-populated. Traceable records for regulators and investors, without a dedicated reporting team.

Field Billing

Invoice the moment the job closes.

Field tickets, POs, and time logs auto-assembled into invoices and GL entries in your ERP. The step that used to take days happens before the crew is back on site.

Contracts

Buried contract terms, surfaced in seconds.

Scanned leases, farm-out agreements, and JIBs read by AI, key obligations, deadlines, and royalty terms returned in plain language. Stop missing clauses buried in 400-page documents.

Field Dispatch

The right crew at the right well, automatically.

Dynamic crew and equipment dispatch based on live alerts, well priorities, road closures, and weather. The optimal schedule recalculates itself, no dispatcher hunting through spreadsheets at 5am.

Procurement

Know your contractor's cert status before they arrive.

Safety records, insurance, and certifications tracked across every vendor. Expiring certs flagged automatically. Riskier contractors identified before the job starts.

Training

Every SOP, explained to someone new.

A conversational AI trained on your own procedures and field notes. New operators and engineers ask questions in plain language and get answers grounded in your actual systems, not generic manuals.
