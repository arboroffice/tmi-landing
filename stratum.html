Why We Exist

Built for the 80% of work that never touches a desk.

Most software was built for offices. We built for the truck, the job site, the rig — and the admin that piles up when nobody in the field has time to type.

01 — Voice First

Nobody on a job site wants to type.

TMI is voice-native. Log a job update, flag a material shortage, report a site issue — hands-free, from wherever the work is. Your crew speaks it. The system captures it, routes it, and acts on it.

🎙 Speak it. Done.
02 — Agentic

Admin finishes in the field, before it reaches an office.

Our agentic systems close the loop automatically. By the time the crew leaves a job, the report is filed, the invoice is drafted, and the next dispatch is queued. Nothing waits for a person to move it.

⚡ Zero manual handoffs
03 — Living Machine

Not software you click. A machine that runs.

Reactive software waits for someone to push a button. TMI is an agentic layer sitting on top of your operation — connecting your filing cabinets, your crews, and your data into a single brain that acts, not just stores.

🧠 Always on. Always moving.

How We Work

We do not have packages on this page.

Every business we work with is different. The size is different. The leaks are different. The goals are different. Showing you a menu of options and a price list would be the wrong way to start.

What we do is this. We come in and look at how your business actually runs. Where the time goes. Where the money leaks. Where the gaps are between what you have now and what your operation could look like on a fully agentic system.

Then we build it. Custom. For you. And we install it.

Some clients need one thing fixed fast. Some need the whole operation rebuilt from the ground up. Some want to own the infrastructure forever. Some want to license what we build to others in their industry.

We figure all of that out on a call.

When TMI is done, your business does not need you to be everywhere anymore. Calls get answered. Leads get followed up. Jobs get dispatched. Reports get filed. Compliance gets tracked. Revenue gets collected. Without you touching any of it.
