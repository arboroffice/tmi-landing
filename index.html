The enemy

You don't have a software problem. You have an operating system problem.

Your company may be profitable, but if every answer, approval, relationship, and decision lives with you, the company still depends on you. That is not fixed by more software. It is disconnected tools, manual work, and knowledge trapped in people's heads, all held together by you. It is the ceiling on how far you can grow and what the company is worth the day you want to step back.

Growth does not create more chaos
