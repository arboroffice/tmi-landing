The real problem

Most businesses don't have a people problem. They have a coordination problem.

Information is scattered. Systems don't talk to each other. Knowledge lives inside people's heads. Teams wait for answers, customers wait for responses, managers wait for approvals, and the owner becomes the bridge holding it all together. That dependency becomes the bottleneck.
