What It Is

The last mile of data, solved.

Every digital transformation effort eventually hits the same wall: the data doesn't exist until someone enters it. Field crews with paper checklists, photos texted to the office, and verbal updates create a data vacuum that every downstream system depends on someone filling manually. Field data capture eliminates that gap - data enters the system the moment it's created, in the field, without re-entry.

Technicians use what they're already carrying: a phone. They tap through a form, take a photo, or speak a note. The system structures it, tags it to the job and location, and syncs it before the van pulls out of the driveway. The office sees it in real time. Invoicing, compliance, and maintenance systems have what they need before the crew reaches the next job.

How It Works

Capture. Structure. Sync.

01

Mobile capture - tap, photo, or voice

Field crews complete forms on a phone, take geotagged photos, or describe the job verbally. The system meets them where they are - no training required, no new behavior patterns to build. Three ways to capture, one destination.

02

Automatic structuring

Voice notes transcribed and structured into work order fields. Photos tagged by job, location, and timestamp. Form data validated and linked to the corresponding record. No admin step between capture and the office seeing it.

03

Real-time sync to downstream systems

Structured field data flows to invoicing, maintenance, compliance, and project management systems as it arrives. Work orders close automatically. Invoices trigger. Safety records update. The office operates on live data, not yesterday's paper.
