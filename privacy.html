Privacy Policy

Last updated: June 8, 2026

Introduction

TMI Technology, LLC ("TMI," "we," "us," or "our") operates the website located at tmitechai.com and provides related services. This Privacy Policy explains how we collect, use, disclose, and safeguard your information when you visit our website or engage with our services.

By using our website or services, you agree to the collection and use of information in accordance with this policy. If you do not agree with the terms of this policy, please do not access the site or use our services.

Information We Collect

Information you provide directly. We collect information you voluntarily provide when you fill out forms on our site, request a consultation, complete our operations audit, or contact us. This may include your name, business name, email address, phone number, and details about your business operations.

Information collected automatically. When you visit our website, we may automatically collect certain information about your device, including information about your web browser, IP address, time zone, and some of the cookies installed on your device. As you browse the site, we also collect information about the individual web pages you view, what websites or search terms referred you to our site, and information about how you interact with the site.

Visitor identification. We use third-party visitor-identification technology that may identify individual business visitors to our website even when you have not submitted a form. This technology can associate your visit with professional information such as your name, job title, employer, company website, professional (LinkedIn) profile, and business email address. We use this information to understand which businesses are interested in our services and to follow up with relevant outreach. This identification currently applies to visitors located in the United States.

Communications. If you contact us by email, phone, or through forms on the site, we retain those communications in order to process your inquiries and improve our services.

How We Use Your Information

We use the information we collect to:

  • Respond to inquiries and provide requested services
  • Send you information you have requested about our services
  • Follow up on audit submissions and consultation requests
  • Improve our website and services based on usage patterns
  • Comply with legal obligations
  • Protect against fraud and abuse

We do not sell your personal information for monetary consideration. However, as described in the "Advertising and Retargeting" section below, we do share certain identifiers with advertising platforms so that we can show our ads to people who have visited our site, which may be considered "sharing" for cross-context behavioral advertising under some privacy laws.

Advertising and Retargeting

We use advertising and analytics tools, including the Meta (Facebook and Instagram) Pixel, to measure the effectiveness of our marketing and to show our ads to people who have visited our website. To do this, we may share a hashed (one-way encrypted) version of identifiers such as your email address with advertising platforms to create "custom audiences" for retargeting. These platforms match the hashed identifiers against their own user records to display our ads to you and to people with similar profiles.

You can control or opt out of this advertising. You may adjust your ad preferences directly with the relevant platform (for example, in your Meta account settings), use industry opt-out tools such as the Digital Advertising Alliance (optout.aboutads.info) or Network Advertising Initiative (optout.networkadvertising.org), or contact us at support@tmitechai.com to ask that we remove you from our advertising audiences and visitor records.

Information Sharing

We may share your information with third-party service providers who assist us in operating our website and delivering our services. These providers are contractually required to keep your information confidential and are prohibited from using it for any purpose other than providing services to us. Categories of service providers include:

  • Email delivery and communication services
  • Database and cloud storage providers
  • Analytics providers
  • Advertising and visitor-identification platforms
  • Scheduling and booking tools

We may also disclose your information if required to do so by law or in the good-faith belief that such action is necessary to comply with legal obligations, protect and defend our rights or property, or protect the safety of our users or the public.

Data Retention

We retain personal information for as long as necessary to fulfill the purposes outlined in this policy, unless a longer retention period is required or permitted by law. Inquiry and audit form data is retained for up to 24 months. You may request deletion of your data at any time by contacting us at support@tmitechai.com.

Cookies

Our website may use cookies and similar tracking technologies to track activity on our website and hold certain information. You can instruct your browser to refuse all cookies or to indicate when a cookie is being sent. If you do not accept cookies, some features of our website may not function properly.

We use cookies and similar technologies for functional purposes, to understand usage patterns, and to support advertising and retargeting as described in the "Advertising and Retargeting" section above. Some of these technologies, including the Meta Pixel and our visitor-identification provider, may be used for targeted advertising and cross-site or cross-context tracking. You can manage cookies through your browser settings and control advertising as described above.

Your Rights

Depending on your location, you may have certain rights regarding your personal information, including the right to access, correct, or delete the personal information we hold about you. To exercise any of these rights, contact us at support@tmitechai.com. We will respond to all requests within 30 days.

Security

We take reasonable measures to protect the security of your personal information. However, no method of transmission over the Internet or electronic storage is 100% secure. While we strive to use commercially acceptable means to protect your information, we cannot guarantee absolute security.

Third-Party Links

Our website may contain links to third-party websites. We have no control over and assume no responsibility for the content, privacy policies, or practices of any third-party sites. We encourage you to review the privacy policy of every site you visit.

Children's Privacy

Our services are not directed to individuals under the age of 18. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children. If you become aware that a child has provided us with personal information, please contact us at support@tmitechai.com.

Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. We will notify you of any changes by posting the new policy on this page with an updated date. We encourage you to review this policy periodically.
