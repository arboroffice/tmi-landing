No. We answer in your company's name, follow your scripts, and handle calls the way your front desk would. To the caller, it is your team picking up. That is the whole point.

Is it humans or AI?

Real people, backed by AI. A human handles the conversation so it feels natural and objections get handled live. AI powers the speed: instant missed-call text-back, capturing details, and routing. You get the warmth of a person with the reliability of a system.

What about after-hours and weekends?

Covered. Those are the calls that usually slip and the ones competitors miss. We answer them so you capture leads while everyone else is closed.

Within days. Once we learn your business and connect your line and calendar, we are answering calls and booking leads. There is no long onboarding.

Do you book straight into my calendar?

Yes. Qualified leads go right onto your calendar or get routed to the right person on your team, with all the details they need. No back and forth.
